# Mathematics education

The [Causality and Kinematic Space curriculum](content/curricula/causality-and-kinematic-space/curriculum.json) owns the lesson sequence, mathematical objectives, briefs, reports, and sources. [MathematicsInRelation](channels/mathematics-in-relation/config.json) supplies the NotebookLM video settings and YouTube publication schedule. Its publication state lives in the channel directory.

A published video is one lesson output. Course completion requires the mathematical work described by the curriculum.
