# Mathematics education

The [Causality and Kinematic Space curriculum](content/curricula/causality-and-kinematic-space/curriculum.json) owns the lesson sequence, mathematical objectives, briefs, reports, and sources. [MathematicsInRelation](channels/mathematics-in-relation/config.json) supplies the Gemini Notebook video settings and YouTube publication schedule. Its publication state lives in the channel directory.

A published video is one lesson output. Course completion requires the mathematical work described by the curriculum.

The section also owns the recovered manuscript of [*The Whirling Crystal*](content/books/the-whirling-crystal/ebook.json). Its twelve Markdown carriers were recovered from the historical 2026-09-03 EPUB because the source manuscript was no longer present in the tree. The historical EPUB and PDF remain untracked edition state under `data/state/editions/the-whirling-crystal/`; they are fabricated renditions awaiting editorial proof, a cover, and an `EbookEditionAcceptance`, not an accepted edition.
