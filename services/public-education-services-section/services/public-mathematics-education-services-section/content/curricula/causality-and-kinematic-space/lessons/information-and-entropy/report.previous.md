# Information, entropy, and kinematic geometry

## Teaching question

When derivatives and combinations of entanglement entropy determine geometric
quantities on kinematic space, what is actually being measured—and which
information-theoretic statements must remain distinct?

## Source-grounded synthesis

Ryu and Takayanagi propose that, in static holographic settings, the
entanglement entropy of a boundary region is proportional to the area of a
corresponding bulk minimal surface. Hubeny, Rangamani, and Takayanagi extend
the prescription covariantly using extremal surfaces in time-dependent
asymptotically AdS spacetimes. These are precise holographic claims with
gravitational, semiclassical, and boundary-theory assumptions. They are not a
general identity between information and ordinary geometric area.

For a boundary interval labeled by endpoints `(u,v)`, Czech, Lamprou,
McCandlish, and Sully construct a kinematic form from mixed endpoint
derivatives of its entanglement entropy. Infinitesimal conditional mutual
information supplies a finite-difference interpretation of that local density.
Positivity properties then support the relevant orientation or measure in the
appropriate setting. Integrating the Crofton form over families of geodesics
reconstructs bulk lengths.

Entropy itself, an entropy derivative, mutual information, conditional mutual
information, a Crofton density, and a bulk length are therefore linked through
a chain of definitions and holographic correspondences. They are not synonyms.
The chain is valuable precisely because each map can be inspected, tested, and
limited.

## Worked finite difference

Take adjacent small boundary segments `A`, `B`, and `C`. The conditional mutual
information
`I(A:C|B) = S(AB) + S(BC) - S(B) - S(ABC)`
compares how the correlation between the outer pieces changes when the middle
piece is known. In the continuum interval parameterization, an appropriately
scaled infinitesimal version is associated with the mixed second derivative of
interval entropy. The equation does not say that every entropy difference is a
spacetime distance; the geometric reading uses the cited state and duality.

## Scope limits and negative control

- The entropy-area prescriptions require holographic and semiclassical
  assumptions and may need quantum or higher-curvature corrections beyond the
  cited leading forms.
- Regulator dependence and endpoint limits must be handled before treating
  entropy derivatives as finite observables.
- Conditional mutual information is an information quantity before any
  geometric interpretation is imposed.
- Invalid inference: “more entropy always means more physical distance.” The
  relevant geometric quantity may depend on derivatives, combinations,
  normalization, state, and reconstruction domain.

## Cinematic direction

Start with an interval entropy function as a translucent surface over endpoint
space. Take a mixed derivative to reveal a local density. Assemble four nearby
entropy terms into conditional mutual information, then tile those densities
over the geodesics intersecting a bulk curve until their integral becomes its
length. Keep labels on every map and break the chain wherever a holographic
hypothesis is removed.
