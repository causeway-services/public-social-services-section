## What this analyzes

A person newly diagnosed or suspecting long COVID or myalgic encephalomyelitis needs to understand the condition, evaluate treatment claims, and find a path to management or recovery, without medical training to filter conflicting claims. The job is "help me understand what's wrong with me and what actually helps, in a form I can absorb on a bad-symptom day."

This note maps the incumbents fulfilling that job, classifies them, measures their reach and citation discipline, and identifies the structural gap in the market. The analysis applies to products the receiver could use *instead* of the health-education-services-section's future channel/ebook/article line — it excludes clinical-trial platforms and provider-facing tools.

The corpus grounding for infection-associated chronic illness exists on disk: the [infection-associated-chronic-illness glossary term](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/natural-science-services-section/services/biological-science-services-section/services/medical-science-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/infection-associated-chronic-illness.md) (243 lines, cited to NASEM 2024 workshop and NIH PAR-25-116), and the [long-covid-services-section predication-literature-review](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/natural-science-services-section/services/biological-science-services-section/services/medical-science-services-section/services/long-covid-services-section/content/text/theories/predication-literature-review-long-covid-mechanisms.md) (653 lines). Both are source-only, not yet turned into public artifacts.

All figures in this note were observed 2026-09-01 (UTC), pulled by direct fetch of first-party pages unless marked otherwise.

**Pattern (observed, not inferred):** The three largest channels by audience (Agle 74K, Medinger 59K, CFS Health 44K) split cleanly on citation discipline. Medinger is the outlier who combines a large audience with visible sourcing to peer-reviewed literature. Agle and CFS Health, the two largest, do not cite primary literature per-video and both monetize a proprietary program. A Goldsmiths University study of Agle's channel drew documented rebuttal on the Science for ME patient-researcher forum, characterizing the channel's ~300 videos as a self-selected sample of people who already believe in psychosomatic/brain-retraining framing, studying only successful recoveries, and functioning in part as marketing for a paid course.

Institutional channels (RECOVER, Dysautonomia International's site) sit at the opposite pole: citation-disciplined but tiny audiences (1.4K–17K subscribers). The institutional layer structurally cannot produce cadence or reach.

The institutional pattern is consistent across modalities: citation-disciplined resources exist, but they live at negligible reach (RECOVER at 1.4K subscribers, Dysautonomia at 17.1K, Bateman Horne at 17.4K). The three largest channels by audience — where a newly diagnosed person is most likely to land — pair large reach with weak citation discipline and either recovery-narrative selection bias or minimal sourcing per-video.

## The structural gap

A receiver searching "long COVID explained" or "what is ME/CFS" discovers, within the first three YouTube results, channels that combine large audiences with minimal citation practice. This is not a bug in any individual channel's strategy — it is the shape of the market. Medinger's channel demonstrates that combining large audience with visible sourcing is possible, but at 59K subscribers he is the only entrant in that position; Agle at 74K outranks him.

The gap is not "more content" — it is "large-audience content with citation discipline that does not collapse into recovery-narrative selection bias." This is the structural position the health-education-services-section must occupy. A merely competent channel at 15K subscribers would fill a gap in absolute quality but would leave the newcomer defaulting to Agle or CFS Health. A competent channel that reaches 50K+ viewers per month while citing the literature it draws from would displace the incumbents from the discovery funnel.

## What would falsify this

This analysis rests on three claims that can fail:

1. **Reach and citation discipline are both measurable and both matter.** If citation discipline proves uncorrelated with what a newly diagnosed person actually retains or acts on, the analysis misweights the design challenge. A future study of viewer retention and comprehension by channel would settle this; until then it is inference from the principle that a confused person with a trusted source outperforms a well-informed person confronting contradictory claims.

2. **The three largest channels actually do lag in citation.** The measurements stand as stated; a systematic recount or a channel's own publication of their citation methodology could revise this. (This is also the weakest claim — it rests on spot-check observation of 3–5 videos per channel, not exhaustive review.)

3. **The receiver's job is what this note claims.** The dossier grounding states the job as "help me understand what's wrong with me and what actually helps, in a form I can absorb on a bad-symptom day." If the actual receiver job is different — if what they actually want is hope/hope-marketing, or social community, or a specific pacing protocol regardless of mechanism — the whole position collapses. A user-research study with newly diagnosed people would test this; prior to that, the job statement rests on proxy evidence (forum posts, comment sections, the stated mission of existing channels).

## Role in the health-education program

The health-education-services-section is at Step 1 of founding: fleet repair and science-base establishment (per the [section README](../../../../../../../../README.md)). Step 2 (channel identity) and Step 3 (production) remain unmade. This note supplies the market position that production must defend: the competitive advantage is reach + citation discipline at the scale Medinger demonstrates is possible but currently occupies alone.

Whether the channel achieves this position is a measurement question, not a design question. The program's role is to build the capability to answer it empirically: can the YouTube, literature, and fabrication primitives named in the README actually compose a 50K+-reach video product that cites its sources and reads as trustworthy to a person reading on a bad day?
