# A study plan {.unnumbered}

The certificate runs six two-week courses, about three months in all. This
plan fits the book to that pace and spaces your practice, following two
findings: recalling from memory outlasts rereading [@roediger2006], and the
best gap between reviews grows with how long you need to remember
[@cepeda2006].

## The rule

Read each chapter at the start of its course, and Chapter 7's entry for that
course before you begin its project. Answer its Check yourself
questions from memory the next day, again about four days later, and again
about ten days later, in the following course. Reread only what you missed.
Then apply the chapter's main tool to your own organization; using a
framework from memory is retrieval practice too.

## The schedule

| Weeks | Course | Read | Review and practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | LSM311 Value Creation | Chapter 1; Chapter 7's LSM311 checklist | Chapter 1 on days 1, 4, 11; value created for one of your products; your five forces |
| 3–4 | LSM312 Change and Disruption | Chapter 2; Chapter 7's LSM312 checklist | Chapter 2 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 1 once; a four-scenario grid for your industry |
| 5–6 | LSM313 Synergies and Alliances | Chapter 3; Chapter 7's LSM313 checklist | Chapter 3 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 2 once; build, borrow or buy for one need |
| 7–8 | LSM314 Business Relationships | Chapter 4; Chapter 7's LSM314 checklist | Chapter 4 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 3 once; a health scorecard for three relationships |
| 9–10 | LSM315 Game Theory | Chapter 5; Chapter 7's LSM315 checklist | Chapter 5 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 4 once; a payoff table for a real rival |
| 11–12 | LSM316 Digital Platforms | Chapter 6; Chapter 7's LSM316 checklist | Chapter 6 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 5 once |

"Once" means one pass through that chapter's questions: older chapters come
back at longer gaps.

## The final review

After the last course, answer every chapter's questions in one sitting,
mixed rather than in order. Then write one page on your organization's
strategy that uses a tool from each chapter: the value it creates, the
change it faces, a combination it should consider, a relationship it depends
on, a rival's likely response, and whether it's, or depends on, a platform. Check each part against its
course's checklist in Chapter 7.
