# About this book {.unnumbered}

This book is a study companion for people taking Cornell University's
Business Management Essentials certificate on eCornell. It follows the
certificate course by course. For each course it states what you should be
able to do when you finish, teaches the ideas through one running business,
and then asks you to recall them before it shows you the answers.

It's for you if you're taking the certificate, deciding whether to, or have
finished it and want a compact review. It assumes no business degree.

## An independent companion

**This book is independent. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or
sponsored by Cornell University or eCornell.** "Cornell" and "eCornell" are
trademarks of Cornell University. The book names them only to say which
certificate it's a companion to.

It doesn't reproduce the courses' videos, readings, tools, discussions or
projects, and it can't stand in for them. You earn the certificate by
completing the courses with their instructors. The book teaches the same
subjects from the research and textbooks the field is built on, and asks its
own questions.

## The certificate at a glance

Cornell's Dyson School offers the certificate online. Read from eCornell on
27 September 2026 [@ecornell-bme-2026]: about three months at four to six
hours a week; six two-week, instructor-led courses taken in order; 72
professional development hours (7.2 CEUs); and a list price of $3,900, with
payment plans. It also includes a six-month pass to eCornell's live AI
workshops, which this book doesn't cover.

## The running example

> **Harbor Street Bakery.** A bakery-café with three shops in a mid-sized
> city. It sells bread, pastries and coffee, employs 38 people, and last year
> took $2 million in revenue. The owner is deciding whether to grow by
> selling bread wholesale to grocery stores and other cafés, and has asked
> her managers to make the case, one discipline at a time.

Each chapter looks at the same decision through its own lens: the numbers,
the customers, the strategy, the organization, the people, and the wider
world.

## How to use this book

Two findings from learning research shape the book. Recalling something from
memory strengthens it more than rereading it [@roediger2006]. Reviews spread
over time beat the same reviews crammed together, and the best gap grows with
how long you need to remember [@cepeda2006]. A large review of study
techniques rated these two, practice testing and spaced practice, the most
useful of the ten it examined, and rated rereading and highlighting among the
least [@dunlosky2013].

Each chapter opens with **What you will be able to do** and ends with **Check
yourself** questions and their **Answers**. Answer on paper before you look,
then come back to the same questions a few days later and again after a week;
the study plan fits that schedule to the certificate's two-week courses. When
a chapter works through Harbor Street, do the same analysis for your own
organization: the courses ask you to.

## What this book doesn't do

- It doesn't grant, or count toward, the Cornell certificate or its
  professional development hours.
- It doesn't replace the courses' instructors, discussions or tools.
- It isn't financial, legal or tax advice for your business.
- It reflects the courses as described on 27 September 2026. If a course has
  changed, trust the course.
