# A study plan {.unnumbered}

The certificate runs six two-week courses in order, about three months in
all. This plan fits the book to that pace and spaces your practice, following
two findings: recalling from memory outlasts rereading [@roediger2006], and
the best gap between reviews grows with how long you need to remember
[@cepeda2006].

## The rule

Read each chapter at the start of its course. Answer its Check yourself
questions from memory the next day, again about four days later, and again
about ten days later, which falls in the following course. Reread only what
you missed. Then apply the chapter to your own organization, as the courses
ask; applying an idea from memory is retrieval practice too.

## The schedule

| Weeks | Course | Read | Review and practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | DYS551 Finance and Accounting | Chapter 1 | Chapter 1 on days 1, 4, 11; calculate five ratios for your organization or a public company |
| 3–4 | DYS552 Marketing | Chapters 2 and 7 (marketing part) | Chapter 2 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 1 once; draft your marketing brief against the checklist |
| 5–6 | DYS553 Strategy and Planning | Chapter 3 | Chapter 3 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 2 once; a SWOT with crossed quadrants |
| 7–8 | DYS554 Managing Organizations | Chapters 4 and 7 (organizational part) | Chapter 4 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 3 once; draft your organizational brief |
| 9–10 | DYS555 Leading Without Authority | Chapter 5 | Chapter 5 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 4 once |
| 11–12 | DYS556 The Global Landscape | Chapter 6 | Chapter 6 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 5 once |

"Once" means one pass through that chapter's questions: older chapters come
back at longer gaps.

## The final review

In the week after the last course, answer every chapter's questions in one
sitting, mixed rather than in order. Then explain one decision in your own
organization through all six lenses, as the book did for Harbor Street's
wholesale line: the numbers, the customers, the strategy, the organization,
the people and the wider world.
