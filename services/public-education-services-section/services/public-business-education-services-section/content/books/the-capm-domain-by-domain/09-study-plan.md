# A study plan {.unnumbered}

This plan spaces your practice across the weeks before your exam. It follows
two findings: retrieving from memory builds more durable learning than
rereading [@roediger2006], and the best gap between reviews grows with how
long you need to remember [@cepeda2006].

## The rule

After each chapter, answer its Check yourself questions from memory, on
paper: the next day, three or four days later, and about a week after that.
Reread only the sections behind the questions you missed. Drop a chapter from
the rotation once you answer all of its questions correctly twice in a row,
until the final review.

## Six weeks to the exam

| Week | Read | Review |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chapters 1 and 2 (Domain 1, 36%) | Each chapter's questions on days 1 and 4 |
| 2 | Chapter 3 (Domain 2, 17%); the formula sheet | Chapter 3 on days 1 and 4; Chapters 1–2 once |
| 3 | Chapter 4 (Domain 3, 20%) | Chapter 4 on days 1 and 4; Chapter 3 and the formula sheet once |
| 4 | Chapters 5 and 6 (Domain 4, 27%) | Each on days 1 and 4; Chapter 4 once |
| 5 | The practice exam (Chapter 7), timed | Score by domain; restudy any domain below 75%; mixed review of all chapters' questions |
| 6 | No new reading | Two mixed reviews of all chapters' questions; the formula sheet daily; rest the day before |

Mixed review means shuffling questions from different chapters together.
That makes you decide which idea each question needs, as the real exam does.

## With less time

With three weeks, read two chapters a week, keep the day-1 and day-4
reviews, and take the practice exam at the start of week 3. With more than
six weeks, stretch the gaps: review each chapter again two and four weeks
after reading it.

## On the day

The real exam has 150 questions in 3 hours, with an optional 10-minute break
after question 75; after the break you can't return to the first half
[@pmi-capm-eco-2023]. Pace yourself at about 72 seconds a question, flag
questions to revisit before the break, and answer every question: unanswered
questions can't score.
