# A study plan {.unnumbered}

This plan spaces your practice across the weeks you spend on the certificate.
It follows two findings: recalling from memory outlasts rereading
[@roediger2006], and the best gap between reviews grows with how long you need
to remember [@cepeda2006].

## The rule

After each chapter, answer its Check yourself questions from memory: the next
day, about three days later, and about a week after that. Reread only what
you missed. Then make the chapter's artifact for your own portfolio project
without looking at the worked example; doing a method from memory is
retrieval practice too.

## A schedule at about ten hours a week

The weeks follow Google's hour estimates; adjust to your pace and keep the
review gaps.

| Weeks | Course | Read | Review and practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 1 Foundations (12 h) | Chapter 1 | Chapter 1 on days 1, 4, 11 |
| 3–4 | 2 Empathize, define, ideate (20 h) | Chapters 2 and 3 | Each on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 1 once; make an empathy map, persona, journey map and problem statement |
| 5–6 | 3 Wireframes and low fidelity (12 h) | Chapter 4 | Chapter 4 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapters 2–3 once; paper wireframes for three screens |
| 7–8 | 4 Research and testing (15 h) | Chapter 5 | Chapter 5 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 4 once; write a research plan and run a study with five people |
| 9–10 | 5 High fidelity (19 h) | Chapter 6 | Chapter 6 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 5 once; check your mockups' contrast |
| 11–12 | 6 Responsive web (15 h) | Chapter 7 | Chapter 7 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 6 once; sketch three breakpoints |
| 13–14 | 7 Social good and portfolio (13 h) | Chapter 8 | Chapter 8 on days 1, 4, 11; write one case study in the seven-part structure |

"Once" means one pass through that chapter's questions in those weeks: older
chapters come back at longer gaps.

## The final review

After the last course, answer every chapter's questions in one sitting, mixed
rather than in order. Then walk through one of your projects aloud, stage by
stage, naming the method you used at each stage and why.

## Before interviews

Be ready to present one case study in five minutes and to answer "What would
you change?" and "How did testing change your design?" with specific
examples.
