# A study plan {.unnumbered}

This plan spaces your practice across the weeks you spend on the certificate.
It follows two findings: recalling from memory outlasts rereading
[@roediger2006], and the best gap between reviews grows with how long you
need to remember [@cepeda2006].

## The rule

After each chapter, answer its Check yourself questions from memory: the
next day, about three days later, and about a week after that. Reread only
what you missed. For the chapters with formulas, queries and code (2, 3, 4, 5
and 7), also retype one worked example from memory and run it; recalling how
to do something is retrieval practice too.

## A schedule at about ten hours a week

The weeks follow Google's hour estimates for each course; adjust to your
pace and keep the review gaps.

| Weeks | Course | Read | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 1 Foundations (13 h) | Chapter 1 | Chapter 1 on days 1, 4, 11 |
| 3–4 | 2 Ask (15 h) | Chapter 2 | Chapter 2 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 1 once |
| 5–6 | 3 Prepare (19 h) | Chapter 3 | Chapter 3 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 2 once |
| 7–8 | 4 Process (16 h) | Chapter 4 | Chapter 4 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 3 once |
| 9–11 | 5 Analyze (26 h) | Chapter 5 | Chapter 5 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapters 3–4 once |
| 12–13 | 6 Share (19 h) | Chapter 6 | Chapter 6 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapter 5 once |
| 14–16 | 7 Python (27 h) | Chapter 7 | Chapter 7 on days 1, 4, 11; Chapters 4–5 once |
| 17 | 8 Capstone (11 h) | Chapter 8 | Chapter 8's checklist on each phase of your case study |

"Once" means one pass through that chapter's questions in those weeks: older
chapters come back at longer gaps.

## The final review

After the capstone, answer every chapter's questions in one sitting, mixed
rather than in order, then rewrite the Maple County analysis from memory in
the tool you'll use most at work: a query, a pivot table or a notebook.

## Before interviews

Practice explaining your case study in two minutes: the question, the data,
one cleaning decision and why, the finding, and the recommendation. Be ready
to write a short `GROUP BY` query and a `VLOOKUP` or `XLOOKUP` by hand.
