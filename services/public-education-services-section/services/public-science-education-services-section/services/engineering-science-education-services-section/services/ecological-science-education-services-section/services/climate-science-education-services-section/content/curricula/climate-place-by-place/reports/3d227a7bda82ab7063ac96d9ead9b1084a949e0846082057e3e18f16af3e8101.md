## Reanalysis-backed history, 1981–2025

NASA POWER's MERRA-2-backed monthly service reports an annual mean near-surface temperature of **8.39 °C** for the first ten complete years and **10.45 °C** for the most recent ten. Their difference is **2.06 °C**. A simple least-squares line through the 45 annual values is **0.57 °C per decade**. Recent mean daily precipitation is **2.65 mm/day**.

These are gridded reanalysis values, not a local station record. They describe the model grid cell containing the place coordinates and can differ from lived conditions, especially near water, mountains, dense urban surfaces, or sparse observing networks.

## Conditional CMIP6 outlook

The comparison uses three HighResMIP/CMIP6 models processed and bias-corrected by Open-Meteo. Each model's 2040–2049 output is compared with its own 2011–2020 baseline. The available ensemble behaves approximately like a high-emissions pathway and is not a probability-weighted forecast.

| Model | 2011–2020 mean °C | 2040–2049 mean °C | Change °C |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CMCC_CM2_VHR4 | 10.21 | 11.89 | 1.68 |
| EC_Earth3P_HR | 10.02 | 11.82 | 1.80 |
| MPI_ESM1_2_XR | 9.80 | 12.10 | 2.30 |

Across these three models, local mean-temperature change ranges from **1.68 to 2.30 °C**, with a median of **1.80 °C**. This range is model spread within one processed ensemble, not a complete uncertainty interval.

## What can and cannot be concluded

- The historical calculation supports a directional account of change in the reanalysis grid cell; it does not prove why any single event happened.
- The projection comparison is conditional on model structure, bias correction, spatial downscaling, and a high-emissions-like pathway.
- City-scale heat can be amplified by land cover and urban form that these model grids do not fully resolve. Precipitation is especially uncertain at local and storm scales.
- No model row is privileged as the prediction. The spread should remain visible in narration.
