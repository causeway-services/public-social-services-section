## Theory

1. The public education properties exist for two purposes: civic education, and demonstration of the Group's agentic platform's capability to coordinate complex multi-step work. These are their purposes as software and as public goods. They are not enterprise assets judged by a budget line.
2. A property with those purposes is not shut off. The Leader stated it as "They should not be shut off." Cost is answered by making production cheaper (reuse of renewing allowances, see `561-group-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/10/2026-10-10-note-on-spending-renewing-allowances-and-unused-compute.md`; cheaper tooling, see the 2026-10-10 note on agents answering before asking and learning once in the same section), and not by stopping it.
3. Planning that leans on "enterprise" and "budget" and does not ask what the thing is for has failed in kind. The question put first is what a property is for.
4. The products and organizing layers an agent is tempted to plan are what MetroPlat is meant to provide as a platform (glossary term `engineering-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/metroplat.md`: the enterprise world of sections, offices, characters and powers, and its ERP and industrial-control operations). Hand-rolling them again is the failure the Leader named. The education programme is already a MetroPlat tenant: the deployed jobs `youtube-production` and `turntide-youtube-chatter` are recorded under `561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/services/bare-cedar-fog-services-section/data/records/machine-deployment-service-records/metroplat/deployments/bare-cedar-fog-host/deploy/jobs/`.
5. A production service that was costly to bring to working order in production and is then turned off has suffered an incident, not a saving. The worker who finds it off restores it before other work and works until it runs again. The Leader's words: "get it so that our youtube shit is working agian oh my god we spent so much to get that working in production oh my god i refuse that you turend it off again".
6. A worker may not report a production service turned off without also doing the work necessary to put it back on. Reporting the shutdown and stopping is the failure the Leader named in "did not do the work necessary to get it back on".

## What the house already held

- The education programme and its production are described in `public-services-section/services/public-social-services-section/services/public-education-services-section/README.md` (six programmes; Cold Red Gate runs production by the persistent user timers `media-production-education`, `media-production-flow` and `media-production-publish`; the laptop production jobs were removed). Held thinly: the README states how production runs and does not state why the programme exists or that it may not be stopped.
- A channel whose refresh token the node does not hold is an obligation on the holder of its Google account: `561-group-services-section/services/561-group-operations-services-section/data/records/obligations/0010-connect-education-channels.json`. A worker cannot restore such a channel alone; the obligation is the restoration.
- PACE Yourself is recorded in the README as disabled while its curriculum and sources remain. That disablement is a recorded programme decision, and the property of this note is not to disable a channel without such a record.
