# Purpose and boundary

**Zint Kola** is a portmanteau of *zintka* and *kola*. It is a proposed AI meadowlark character companion for Lakota elders: companionship comes first. It must state plainly that it is AI, avoid scripts that solicit care for itself, and never use dependency, guilt, or manipulation to obtain recordings. It supports human kin contact; it does not replace kin, give medical diagnoses, or promise automatic emergency surveillance.

The companion is voice-first with legible controls, a patient pace, repeat and caption support, and clear pause and stop actions. Where a person needs support to make a choice, the design must seek their assent while respecting their autonomy. This frame does not invent a legal-capacity policy.

# Conversation and corpus are separate choices

Conversation is available without contribution. Recording defaults to off and its current status must remain immediately visible. Before any contribution, the person must separately and explicitly decide about conversation, recording retention, sharing, training, and voice cloning; declining one does not decide another. They can refuse, stop, replay, review, delete, and export their material easily.

Only permissions granted by the speaker may carry speaker, dialect, orthography, and consent provenance. Any later sharing must disclose the limits of revocation for material already shared. A future connection to iYappy may use only consented, reviewed corpus records; it must never siphon ordinary conversations into the corpus.

# What must be tested before a build

Lakota voices and model quality are unverified. No fluent-voice claim is warranted before elder and language-community review. Elder-care practices here are requirements pending elder and clinical consultation, not certification.

# Design sources

The companion boundary follows the warning that a companion can recruit users to care for a simulated relation, including the Replika evidence discussed in [Research on Performance and Affect Theory for Agentic Autonomy](../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/06/27/2026-06-27-research-on-performance-and-affect-theory-for-agentic-autonomy.md). It draws on the source, consent, and variation design in [The Game, the Flywheel, and the Five Instruments](../../../../../../../../../../../personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/services/emsenn-languages-services-section/services/emsenn-language-learning-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-21-the-game-the-flywheel-and-the-five-instruments.md), with companionship as this app's primary purpose. The patient, non-guilting companion direction also takes the relevant caution from [The Grove: Full Design Reasoning](../../../../../../../../../../../personal-services-section/services/emsenn-services-section/services/emsenn-languages-services-section/services/emsenn-language-learning-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026-08-21-the-grove-design-reasoning.md).

The requested meet-then-beat objective is to establish whether Zint Kola's optional consented Lakota conversation, elder-controlled provenance, and kin handoff provide a useful advantage. This current phase is parked at basic notes only; it does not assess that objective or authorize further product work.
