# Elder Companion Services Section

This section holds the public-service frame for **Zint Kola**, an AI meadowlark character companion for Lakota elders. Elder companionship is its primary purpose. It may support a separately optional, elder-controlled contribution of Lakota speech to a reviewed corpus, but conversation must remain available without contributing.

The current frame is the dated [Zint Kola concept note](./content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/02/2026-09-02-zint-kola.md). It creates no runtime, account, recording, deployment, or language-model capability. Lakota voice and model quality remain an unverified prebuild gate, not a claim of fluency.
