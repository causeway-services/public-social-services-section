Proof of Adaptation is a proposed way to support verified, local adaptation
knowledge. It starts from the fact that a useful practice can be reported,
measured, replicated, or merely repeated without evidence—and those are not
the same kind of claim.

# The proposed loop

Signals and research enter a community intake; discussion develops questions;
a durable vault retains sources and provenance; practices are turned into
guides or trials; and witnessed results refine the next round of questions.
The point is not to manufacture certainty. It is to make the evidentiary state
of a claim visible and improve it through repeatable work.

# Work-shaped participation

The design proposes a proof-of-adaptation token earned for completed,
witnessed work and data quality. The proposal expressly does not describe an
investment asset or promise appreciation. Before any such mechanism can be
treated as operational, its custody, admission, and governance conditions must
be earned in the settlement record.
