Turntide Islands is opening a four-week community-programming intake for
September 2026. This is a real invitation to shape and host useful community
work. It is not an invented calendar of meetings that no human agreed to run.

# September intake cycle

## Week 1 — Open Harbor

In `#mainland`, propose an orientation or community gathering, two
availability windows with timezone, and access needs.

## Week 2 — Question Quarry

In `#question-quarry`, offer one practical or research question for a sourced
response or facilitated session.

## Week 3 — Project Dock

After completing the arrival journey, share a project, concrete ask, or bounded
offer in the relevant existing topical community. `#dock` remains reserved for
new arrivals and its established welcome flow.

## Week 4 — Field Notes

In `#location`, share a place-based observation, practice, or source-linked
note, distinguishing observation from inference.

[Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/zHRy3RQgkD) to take part. A proposal can
be asynchronous; nobody has to volunteer as a live host to contribute a
question, project, or field note.

# Propose a gathering

For a synchronous event, post in `#mainland` with:

1. the purpose and desired outcome;
2. two possible start windows and your timezone;
3. expected length and participation format;
4. accessibility needs or accommodations;
5. whether you are willing to host, co-host, or are requesting a host.

A human must accept responsibility before a meeting is scheduled. Discord's
Events list is authoritative for confirmed date, time, voice channel, and RSVP
state. Zelo may explain this process and route proposals, but does not
volunteer, host, moderate, or send automatic reminders on a person's behalf.

# What happens after

Confirmed gatherings receive a named human organizer, reviewed reminder plan,
and closeout. A closeout can record what happened, unanswered questions, useful
links, and whether anything should be rescued into the public knowledge record.
Ordinary conversation is not automatically copied into an archive or analytics
cohort.
