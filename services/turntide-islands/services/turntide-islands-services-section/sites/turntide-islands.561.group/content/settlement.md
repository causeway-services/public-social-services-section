Turntide Islands is a Discord-native community settlement. Discord is one
surface through which the community speaks; it is not the archive of record.
The durable record is designed around typed admissions and refusals,
content-addressed records, and witnessed writes.

This makes the outward-facing rule straightforward: a path is not claimed to
be live merely because it is desirable. When a required admission has not been
made, the record says so. When evidence is not sufficient, the appropriate
result is an honest absence rather than an invented answer.

# The public purpose

The settlement focuses on local and household-scale climate adaptation:
building a usable, shared body of knowledge about what works under particular
conditions. It is intended to strengthen practical response, mutual learning,
and the capacity to act together amid climate instability.

Its proposed knowledge-refinement loop moves from signals and discussion to a
durable knowledge vault, then to guides, trials, and new evidence. That loop
is described in [[proof-of-adaptation|Proof of Adaptation]].

The account above is drawn from the parent settlement's public definition and
working synthesis, with historical promotional language left in its source
record rather than presented here as a current operational claim.
