### Retained formulation


**Turntide Islands** is a virtual space focused on responding, not reacting, to climate weirding.

People gather here to share knowledge and build practices and communities that are grounded in an honest acceptance of the compound crises that affect us, from climate weirding to catabolic collapse.

Turntide Islands exists as a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) using polycentric participatory consensus. Proposals are discussed openly, quorum-based votes determine outcomes, and all governance actions are permanently recorded on-chain for accountability. Each topical area follows the same discussion → quorum vote → recorded decision structure, allowing decentralized yet organized decision-making across the community. (In the future, members may be given tokens in relation to their participation which may be used to weight votes in proposals.)

Turntide Islands was incubated by the 561 Group, another DAO with a mission to pursue Black Liberation and Indigenous Sovereignty.

Turntide Islands is administrated by emsenn, who documents and implements all governance decisions. (In the future, an automation may handle these technical actions.)

To join Turntide Islands, use a link to join the Discord server, and click the :verify: emoji below the welcome message. There are several topical communities which are open to the public, and may be joined by clicking the appropriate emoji.
