# Future DAO deployment specification

Turntide Islands has an established DAO identity and an unresolved historical
record. This specification does not recover, recreate, or claim any historical
wallet, token, treasury, Colony, contract, chain, or balance. The public
registry records the evidence boundary.

## Intended governance cycle

For each proposed decision, the affected polycenter publishes a proposal;
members have a public discussion window; the applicable quorum and electorate
rule are published before voting; eligible people vote; a durable record
captures the tally, decision, dissent, and implementation evidence; a named
human carries out any accepted action; and a reflection-and-appeal period keeps
the record correctable. Cross-community rights, infrastructure, resources, and
constitutional changes require the shared process rather than unilateral local
action.

The existing proposed polycentric protocol supplies the conceptual basis. Its
historical threshold numbers remain hypotheses, not deployment parameters.

## Zelo as a bounded clerical office

If the community admits it, Zelo may validate a proposal form, announce a
window, calculate a transparent tally from authorized input, draft a receipt,
and show pause or appeal status. It does not create or hold keys, select an
electorate, set quorum, vote, sign, execute a decision, decide an appeal, or
upgrade its own authority. A human remains responsible for every transition
from discussion to binding action.

## Admission and safety

No implementation begins before the community admits a chain, a testnet, a
governance platform (including whether to use Colony), custody and recovery
rules, membership and quorum rules, record system, named human safety officers,
and public acceptance criteria. The first exercise is testnet-only. Production
requires a reviewed testnet exercise, a separate production admission,
independent authority/recovery review, and a workable public exit/export path.

Emergency pause can stop automation or execution while humans document the
reason and review it; it cannot erase an appeal. Appeals require a published
window and human disposition receipt. Upgrades require ratified signers,
threshold, notice, rollback, and receipt rules. Members retain ordinary
community access when exiting governance and can export public decision
records.

The machine-readable desired state is intentionally non-executable. It is a
review checklist, not a deployment authorization.
