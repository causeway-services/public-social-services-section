## Community and programming ideas

### The refinement loop

Status: `proposed`; roadmap: R1/R3.

Selected news and member questions enter relevant discussion lanes; useful
discussion is rescued into a durable vault; external agents cross-link and
verify it; the result returns as guides, drills, practices, and better questions
for the next cycle. The loop, not chat volume, is the core product.

### Ordinary retention is legitimate work

Status: `admitted-direction`; roadmap: R1.

Orientation funnels, useful notifications, digests, recurring rituals, member
progression, and consented re-engagement are normal community infrastructure.
Earlier assistant interpretation that critical theory prohibited these tools
was explicitly corrected by Morgan.

### A human-owned programming calendar

Status: `proposed`; roadmap: R1.

- Open Harbor: accessible community gathering and orientation.
- Question Quarry: recurring practical/research questions.
- Project Dock: member projects, asks, and offers.
- Field Notes: reports from places, practices, trials, and observations.
- Clinics and office hours: bounded practitioner/human expert sessions.
- Seasonal exchanges, build challenges, drills, and hack days.

Every recurring program needs a named human owner, accessibility/timezone
information, closeout notes, and a way for members to propose alternatives.

### Emergency knowledge is public

Status: `proposed product invariant`; roadmap: R1/R3.

Safety-critical adaptation information should not be put behind a paid vault.
The content model needs an explicit emergency/public-safety classification
rather than relying on editorial memory.

## Knowledge and adaptation ideas

### Vault, not chat

Status: `admitted-direction`; roadmap: R1/R3.

Discord is a porch. The durable product is exportable, attributed, correctable,
searchable knowledge. The vault should carry reading paths, source trails,
selected conversation rescue, practices, and briefings without treating
ordinary participation as commercial consent.

### Consent-aware conversation rescue

Status: `proposed`; roadmap: R1/R3.

Create a deliberate member workflow to nominate a conversation fragment for the
vault, confirm authorship/visibility/attribution, preserve corrections, and
publish only the admitted projection. A Zelo mention alone is not a license.

### Proof of Adaptation

Status: `whitepaper draft`; roadmap: R3.

Candidate practices pass through conceived, simulated, field-tested,
replicated, and established evidence states. Cheap simulation, hazard, cost,
water, and energy screening should allocate scarce real-world trial effort.
Consented field trials return protocols, observations, measurements, and honest
refusals when evidence is insufficient.

### Practice bounties and field trials

Status: `proposed`; roadmap: R3.

Members could accept clearly bounded, safety-reviewed paid trials for adaptation
practices. Compensation is for completed work and evidence quality—not for
predictive claims, investment returns, or posting.

### Islands Chart

Status: `authored/worldbuilding direction`; roadmap: R3.

Rather than duplicate the operational organism as a game world, expose a
human-readable observer chart: communities, projects, practices, evidence
states, relationships, and open questions become navigable islands. The chart
helps people understand and intervene in the real system; it does not pretend
the representation is the territory.

## Discord and Zelo ideas

### Topic-aware intake routing

Status: `proposed`; roadmap: R3.

Admitted signals can be proposed to matching discussion lanes, with rate limits,
source links, deduplication, and human editorial control. Queue counts and
internal processing jargon must never become community programming.

### Zelo as accountable office

Status: `proposed hardening`; roadmap: R1/R2.

Give Zelo and each public steward persona an office record, named remit,
disclosure, liberties, duties, immunities, source-grounding gates, boundary
gates, and a human escalation owner. Zelo is operational infrastructure, not an
emotional companion or substitute moderator.

### Persona output gates

Status: `proposed`; roadmap: R2.

Compile office duties into code-level checks: witnessed claims, no fabricated
absence, no care-performance, no public commitments beyond authority, no
engagement bait, and explicit withholding when evidence is insufficient.

### Plural identity, character, persona, and masks

Status: `researched`; roadmap: R2.

Meet PluralKit-class system/member profiles, proxying, switches, groups,
privacy, export/API, and moderation attribution, then generalize upstream. See
`plural-identity-character-persona-and-masks.md`.

## Product and funding ideas

### Founding knowledge vault

Status: `product hypothesis; commercial activation not proven`.

Possible value: weekly briefings, retrieval/reading paths, attributed rescue,
and transparent receipts. Historic price points remain hypotheses. Ordinary
community membership and emergency knowledge stay free.

### Locality and relocation knowledge

Status: `proposed`.

Answer bounded, non-personal practical questions about places using sourced
local knowledge and explicit limitations. Do not turn locality knowledge into
extractive risk scoring for institutions that would harm residents.

### Climate signals desk

Status: `proposed`.

Provide timely, witnessed signal feeds and source trails without financial,
medical, relocation, or emergency advice. Content-addressed observation time
could distinguish genuine early observation from retrospective marketing.

### Cohorts, credentials, and office hours

Status: `proposed`.

High-touch learning cohorts, practitioner sessions, and witnessed completion
records may finance public community infrastructure. Credentials represent
observed work, not purchased status.

### Cooperative group buys and tool access

Status: `proposed`.

Pool demand for tools, supplies, seeds, training, or services through explicit
terms, custody, refund, and failure states. The general capability belongs
upstream in settlement commerce; TTI is a possible first customer.

### Work-shaped adaptation credits

Status: `historical administrator proposal; current chain state unverified`.

March 2025 governance discussion described `TTIDAO` as recognition or proof of
adaptation rather than speculative capital. Later transcript work proposed
work-shaped credits for witnessed trials and community uses. The relationship
between these ideas, any deployed token, and current governance is unresolved.
None should be sold or marketed as an investment.

### Admission-gated institutional products

Status: `proposed`.

Libraries, watershed groups, co-ops, tribal governments, mutual-aid networks,
and public resilience offices may be compatible partners. Extractive actuarial
or displacement uses should be able to fail a published buyer admission check.

## Governance ideas

### Legible offices inside DAO governance

Status: `admitted-direction`.

TTI's established self-description and March 2025 administrator messages treat
it as a DAO. The corrective requirement is therefore not to defer the name, but
to make its offices legible: declared authority, duties, holders, receipts, and
appeals. Human office holders and agent sessions remain categorically distinct.

### Contribution-weighted trial governance

Status: `historical thesis with later design variants; current implementation unverified`.

Influence over a field-trial slate could derive from witnessed work and
responsible prior trials rather than speculative purchase. March 2025 messages
described a digital government, `TTIDAO`, a treasury, and a separate
fee-contributing fund; this register has not yet verified their public
identifiers or current operation.
