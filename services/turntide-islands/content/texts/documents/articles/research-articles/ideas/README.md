This directory holds source-bounded ideas for Turntide Islands. It is a place
to remember possibilities without pretending every brainstorm is a roadmap
commitment.

Each idea should record:

- status and roadmap horizon;
- the human or source artifact that introduced it;
- the problem and proposed member outcome;
- reusable upstream capability versus TTI-specific configuration;
- privacy, safety, moderation, and accessibility risks;
- dependencies and a small falsifiable experiment;
- evidence required before admission.

Current entries:

- `2026-08-29-transcript-mined-idea-register.md` — a provenance-indexed,
  deduplicated register of community, programming, adaptation, knowledge,
  governance, funding, public-presence, identity, and operations ideas mined
  from Morgan's Codex and Claude conversations.
- `plural-identity-character-persona-and-masks.md` — meet the valuable
  identity-expression surface of PluralKit, then generalize it without
  conflating plural humans, characters, masks, offices, or AI personas.
- `recognition-not-permission-and-work-shaped-governance.md` — preserve the
  historical TTIDAO thesis that governance should recognize and support
  adaptation work without turning recognition into speculative permission.

Transcript-mined entries must cite exact local artifact paths/session IDs and
dates. They should paraphrase rather than republish private conversational
material. Human review decides whether each candidate becomes its own article,
merges into an existing idea, or remains only in the source index.
