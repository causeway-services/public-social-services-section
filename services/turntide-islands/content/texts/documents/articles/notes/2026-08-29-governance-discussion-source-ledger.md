# Turntide Islands governance discussion source ledger

This ledger reframes durable ideas that emsenn introduced in Turntide Islands'
`#governance-discussion` channel during March 2025. It is not a raw transcript,
a record of ratification, or proof of present implementation. Other
participants' messages and personal circumstances are excluded.

The source channel is
[Turntide Islands #governance-discussion](https://discord.com/channels/1184863861065658419/1346511343573926010).
It was read on 2026-08-29 using the configured administrator identity. The
links below may require membership in the Discord server.

## Governance mechanics proposed

- Voting should support abstention. Quorum answers whether enough of the
  governed community participated; it should not force a yes-or-no preference
  from every participant.
- Governor status could reflect recent participation. The historical proposal
  was to remove the role after a month without conversation and restore it on
  request, making quorum track an active body rather than an old membership
  list.
- Constitutional decisions should require broader participation than ordinary
  decisions. The historical proposal suggested 50% quorum for changes to the
  decision system and 20% for other decisions.
- Decisions should leave immutable, transparent records. Hive was the
  specifically proposed record system in this discussion.
- Zelo's configuration should be documented so the community can understand
  and propose changes to its authority and behavior.
- Governors need a shared discussion space for orienting and planning the
  server's direction.

## Entry, invitation, and delegation

Reaction roles were already understood as part of the intended member journey,
and repairing them was an explicit concern. The administrator proposed an open
invitation posture during the early community period and delegated full
administrative permissions to two people who offered to help. Later messages
used absence of objection within a stated period as a lightweight invitation
decision and delegated the technical ability to create an invite.

## A planning grammar

The discussion distinguishes the following cycle:

`values + position → orientation → direction → strategy and tactics → plan → action → reflection → reconsidered values`

Orientation comes before choosing direction; plans come after strategy and
tactics. Different people can contribute to different parts of the cycle. The
organization is not treated as merely the sum of its members: people jointly
move and maintain a social entity that has continuity, constraints, and effects
of its own.

## Finance as education, not pooled investment

The administrator explicitly distinguished mutual education about finance,
geopolitics, and decentralized networks from operating a hedge fund or making
members' financial decisions. That boundary is durable. The channel also
contains informal, time-bound personal finance and benefits discussion; this
ledger does not republish it as guidance.

## DAO and adaptation thesis

The March 2025 discussion described a digital government with a treasury and a
`TTIDAO` token. It also described a separate fund whose fee revenue could
flow to the treasury while ownership of that fund remained distinct from
governance. The proposed token was a record of recognition for adaptation work,
not speculative capital or permission to participate. Governance would
recognize and support useful work already happening rather than centrally
decide all work in advance.

## Long-range strategy proposed

The historical strategy joined narrative work, decentralized tools, and
regional adaptation:

1. develop a public language for cooperative resilience rather than
   reactionary survivalism;
2. connect existing communities working on decentralized technology,
   permaculture, municipalism, and off-grid systems;
3. move resources toward small-scale resilience work;
4. build forkable, local-first tools for governance, trade, energy,
   communication, and security;
5. test integration in a real region and connect regional hubs without making
   them centrally dependent.
