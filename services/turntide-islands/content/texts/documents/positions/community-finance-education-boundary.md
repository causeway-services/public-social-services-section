# Community finance education boundary

Turntide Islands can discuss contemporary finance, decentralized networks, and
geopolitics as subjects of mutual education. Those systems shape whether
communities can obtain land, food, energy, tools, and time for adaptation, so
understanding them belongs within the wider project.

Turntide Islands is not an organized hedge fund, investment adviser, brokerage,
or vehicle for making members' personal financial decisions. Community
software must not:

- recommend an asset or account as suitable for a particular member;
- infer benefits, tax, disability, income, or asset status from conversation;
- pool member money without separately admitted legal, custody, accounting,
  governance, and exit terms;
- describe a token, treasury, fund, yield, balance, or on-chain organization as
  live without current public evidence;
- market governance participation as an investment return;
- retain sensitive personal circumstances merely because they arose in chat.

Educational work should distinguish sourced explanation, personal experience,
open research questions, and professional advice. High-stakes claims require
current authoritative sources and an explicit limitation. Members remain free
to discuss their experiences; ordinary participation does not authorize the
community to turn those experiences into a financial profile or product.
