#+name: definition
*/[[file:uzdar_brag.org][Uzdar Brag]]/* was a [[file:goblins.org][goblin]] who lived from [[file:29407bc.org][29407bc]] to [[file:29392bc.org][29392bc]], and is remembered for discovering the route from the [[file:undermuck.org][Undermuck]] up into the [[file:widewoods.org][Widewoods]].

#+toc: headlines 2

* Biography

  #+name: uzdar-brag-birth-event
  On the [[file:19th_of_browning_29407bc.org][19th of Browning, 29407bc]], [[file:uzdar_brag.org][Uzdar Brag]] was born in the [[file:undermuck.org][Undermuck]], [[file:worldkeep.org][Worldkeep]], [[file:../teraum.org][Teraum]].

  #+name: uzdar-brag-discovers-route-to-the-widewoods-event
  On the [[file:2nd_of_sleeting_29395bc.org][2nd of Sleeting, 29395bc]], [[file:uzdar_brag.org][Uzdar Brag]] discovered a route from the [[file:undermuck.org][Undermuck]] up into the [[file:widewoods.org][Widewoods]].

  #+name: uzdar-brag-death-event
  On the [[file:9th_of_rotting_29392bc.org][9th of Rotting, 29392bc]], in the [[file:widewoods.org][Widewoods]], [[file:uzdar_brag.org][Uzdar Brag]] tripped on a stick, fell into a mud puddle, drowned, and died.
