** DRAFT Overview
*/Arathel County/* is a marshy region in the northwest /Green Delta/. Its
county seat is the town of /Arathel/, and its principle exports are
/limestone/, /wool/, and /cokeberry/ and /cokeberry/ products.

** DRAFT Settlements
#+TOC: headlines 1 local
*** DRAFT Arathel
    :PROPERTIES:
    :CUSTOM_ID: arathel
    :END:
#+INDEX: Arathel!"Arathel" in /Geography and Settlements of Marby County/
*/Arathel/* is the /human/-populated county seat of Arathel County.
First settled in 478bc as a logging community, Arathel is one of the
larger towns in the community. It is governed by a council of local
commercial leaders. its primary exports are wool, limestone, and
cokeberries. Notably, Arathel was the capital of the Astar Empire. For
more information, see "Arathel" in the /Arathel County Gazetteer/.

*** DRAFT Slow Lightning Inn
    :PROPERTIES:
    :CUSTOM_ID: slow-lightning-inn
    :END:
#+INDEX: Slow Lightning Inn!"Slow Lightning Inn" in /Geography and Settlements of the Arathel County/p
The */Slow Lightning Inn/* is a large single-storey timber-framed
building with a small fenced yard. It is notably clean, and well-lit
inside with a large /iron/ chandelier. Accomodations consist of several
small rooms with wooden cots and several hammocks in the common room.
/Wulfa/, the barkeep, is known for his dried /radish/ chips and his
clearly-embellished adventuring stories.

*** DRAFT Crossed Candles Inn
    :PROPERTIES:
    :CUSTOM_ID: crossed-candles-inn
    :END:
#+INDEX: Crossed Candles Inn!"Crossed Candles Inn" in /Geography and Settlements of the Arathel County/
The */Crossed Candles Inn/* is a simple wooden shack with several
shuttered windows. Accomodations consist of a single large room with
wooden cots. The /Inn/ is said to be haunted by the coast of a /human/
man named /Alfric/. The innkeeper is a short /human/ woman named
/Lexandra Terr/. She is a retired thief, and maintains a collection of
various maps.
**** DRAFT Alfric
     :PROPERTIES:
     :CUSTOM_ID: alfric
     :END:
#+INDEX: Alfric!"Alfric" in /Geography and Settlements of Arathel County/
*/Alfric/* was a /human/ soldier during the /Great Wars/, who was
visited the /Crossed Candles Inn/ on campaign. While there, he tried
to start a bar fight, but slipped a puddle, cracked his head, and
died. Now, his /ghost/ haunts the /Inn/, where he whispers into the
ears of drunken regulars that newcomers have said unkind things about
them and their kin. Visitors to the /Inn/ *risk* being randomly
attacked by a drunken local.

 seem very happy about it.

#+INDEX: Golden Shield Inn!"Golden Shield Inn" in /Geography and Settlements of the Arathel County/pp
The */Golden Shield Inn/* is a large single-storey timber-framed
building, with a reinforced wooden door and unusually high ceilings.
Accomodations consist of a several large rooms with beds and straw
mattresses, and a mezzanine with several wooden cots. The innkeeper is
a young /human/ man named /Athyer/.

passed away in Blooming, 77pc from an illness that Athyer didn't get.
