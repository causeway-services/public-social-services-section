Here's some pages to learn about Teraum. Unless otherwise stated, assume information about Teraum is about the world as it was about 80 years after the [Teraumian Collapse](./encyclopedia/times/teraumian-collapse.md), and information is, unless otherwise specified, presented how the [Red Union](./encyclopedia/groups/red-union.md) does (or would) talk about it.


# Atlas-at-a-Glance

Two major known continents: Eastland and [westland](./encyclopedia/places/westland.md), separated by the Optic Ocean

Eastland has:

- [Central Plains](./encyclopedia/places/central-plains.md)
  - Plains, sandhills, etc.
- Farsteppes
  - Steppes
  - Trading town of [Helmet's Dent](helmet's-dent.md)
  - 
- Farfarsteppes
  - (Usually frozen) steppes
- Green Delta
  - Marshes and farmland
  - Big city of [ack](./encyclopedia/places/ack.md) and its suburb [bellybrush](./encyclopedia/places/bellybrush.md)
  - Four counties: Arathel, Marby, Pled, Sherwyn
- Unseen Sea
  - Vast desert
  - Tayopolis
- [widewoods](./encyclopedia/places/widewoods.md)
- [worldkeepers](./encyclopedia/groups/worldkeepers.md)

Westland has:

- Elflands
  - Dense temperate forests
- [Halfling Country](./encyclopedia/places/halfling-country.md)
  - Rolling hills and farmland
  - Gnalens
  - [Westland Coast](./encyclopedia/places/westland-coast.md)
    - [Trepi Island](./encyclopedia/places/trepi-island.md)

# Factions
- House Oru
  - Self-declared sovereignty in the Farsteppes, led by Renkah Oru and her son Kolpe Oru acting as governor.
  - The Red Union invoices House Ellon for the lands Oru claims, so does not recognize their soveriegnty.
- House Ellon
  - Defunct, territory claimed by Oru. Rumored [Last Baron of House Ellon](./encyclopedia/people/last-baron-of-house-ellon.md)
- [Blacksmith's Guild](blacksmith's-guild.md)
  - [Ackian guild](./encyclopedia/groups/ackian-guild.md) and now [Red Union guild](./encyclopedia/groups/red-union-guild.md) for blacksmithing and other smithing. Headquartered in the [Copper Ward](./encyclopedia/places/copper-ward.md) of Ack.
  - [Atrix Hucklebee](./encyclopedia/people/atrix-hucklebee.md) is a member.
- [Chandler's Guild](chandler's-guild.md)
  - candlemakers and printing press
  - Member of the [Red Union](./encyclopedia/groups/red-union.md)
  - Headquartered in Ack, with offices in Bellybrush, Marby, and Sherwyn
  - Wybert is a member
  - 
- [Cloudbound Trading Company](./encyclopedia/groups/cloudbound-trading-company.md)
  - Transportation company focused on, but not limited to, airship travel. Headquarted in Ack with offices in Bellybrush, Marby, Sherwyn, Arathel, Pled, Helmet's Dent, Gnalens, and Tayopolis.
  - member of the Red Union
  - Stevie was a member, now deceased
- Gnalens is a city but also a faction.
  - democratic republic on the coast of Halfling Country
  - run by [The Governor of Gnalens](./encyclopedia/people/the-governor-of-gnalens.md)
    - has a Lieutenant Governor of Gnalens, currently [Ander Falone](./encyclopedia/people/ander-falone.md)
- Leatherworkers Guild
  - Member of the Red Union
  - Offices in Ack and Gnalens
  - Wayne Helder is a member
- [Red Union](./encyclopedia/groups/red-union.md)
  - Bureaucrats who control many human systems with debt and credit
  - Originated in the [Red Ward](./encyclopedia/places/red-ward.md) of Ack, with offices established in many former temples anyplace humans live.
