#### <span class="org-todo todo DRAFT">DRAFT</span> Scene 1 {#scene-1}

Skah's[^fn:1] heel squeaked as she stopped short before the bend in the corridor, and again as she resumed jogging, after making sure the way was clear. The call from Horticulture had been urgent.

As she went down the way, the lighting began to shift toward that unique purple that characterized the section. Her shoe squeaked again as she stopped at the door, and she took a big breath in as she opened the door.

"Hotah!"[^fn:2] she said, surprised to see him sitting behind the desk. "I thought you were on runner duty like me?"

"Horticulture's trained staff are all busy, so they pulled me away. We're coming up on Ben[^fn:3] faster than expected, so they want to get starters going now. I've got an inventory for you to pull from Storage, here." Hotah slid a clipboard across the desk. Skah reached out to take it.

"Doesn't seem like it needed an 'urgent,'" she commented, skimming the listing. "A lot of apples."

"Some blight on their largest planet," Hotah explained. "Anyplace you're planning to visit when we arrive?"

"Orchards, I guess," Skah replied, smiling. "And you know I'll visit the recreation complexes on Ben-Four. The immersim just can't do those jumps justice."

"Let me know your schedule, I'll come with you."

"Of course. Well," Skah waved the clipboard in her hand. "Better get to this 'urgent' work," she said, sighing with a smile. Hotah grinned back, and turned his attention to the digital display on his desk.


#### <span class="org-todo todo DRAFT">DRAFT</span> Scene 2 {#scene-2}

Skah didn't jog as she made her way from Horticulture to the nearest receiving station, and when she arrived, there was a small group of other crew waiting to use the system. She grabbed a ticket, #76, and looked at the station's display, "69." Nice: not to long of a wait. She went to the bar and smiled at the person waiting behind.

"What can I get for you?" he asked.

"Fruit juice?" she asked, and he nodded, working behind the bar for a moment before setting a glass full of opaque pink juice on the counter.

"Grapefruit and apple," he informed her. _Apple sort of day,_ Skah thought to herself, smiling at the person who served her and taking her glass to a table. Someone was playing the piano at the station, but just as Skah sat down, the station's display made a buzz and switched to "71," and the piano-player got up and went to the station proper.

Someone's kid, maybe 8, brought themselves up to the piano and after getting comfortable on the bench, starting hammering classic melodies played without rhythm. _Sounds out of time_, Skah thought, amusing herself with the wordplay.

The child played for several minutes, before the person with ticket #73 scooped them up and brought them to the corridor, where a handtruck loaded with boxes was waiting. They went down the hall, and Skah returned her glass to the bar. Someone else was standing behind it now, and she smiled at them as they took the glass away.

The station display buzzed again, "76," so Skah went up to the station proper, and looked at her clipboard. She began to punch in the requisition numbers, pressing a button labeled "REQ" after each number. By the time she was on the 20th request, small envelopes began to drop from a chute in the station.

All-in-all, Skah requested 83 envelopes, each containing about two dozen seeds. When she'd received them all, she stepped to the side, and pushed another button on the station. The display buzzed, "77."

Skah grabbed a satchel from the station, and put her envelopes in it as she slung it over her shoulder: a familiar motion. She walked almost back to the corridor, before stopping and returning to the bar.

"Biscuit?" she asked the person behind it, who handed her a scone.

"Blueberry," he said, and she smiled in thanks, before heading on her way back to Horticulture.

#### <span class="org-todo todo PLAN">PLAN</span> Scene 11 {#scene-11}

The Okaga uses its small crafts to try and chase the pirates, but the pirates use the signalling array to leave the system, and saboteurs destroy the array stations before the crafts can follow.


#### <span class="org-todo todo PLAN">PLAN</span> Scene 12 {#scene-12}

Convention Hall. The caucus which voted for the Okaga to stay are accused by the other caucuses of coordinating the robbery. Delegates caught sabotaging the receiving stations are dragged in. People are debating what to do when the Okaga crew comes in.

They announce that the supplies they carry to repair the relay station have been taken, and they have no immediate way to repair them.

In the chaos following the announcement, one of the delegates - the one followed in earlier scenes - is lynched in the convention hall. The Okaga crew then proceeds to detain and deport all in-system residents from the ship.


### <span class="org-todo todo PLAN">PLAN</span> Act 2 {#act-2}


#### <span class="org-todo todo PLAN">PLAN</span> Scene 13 {#scene-13}

It is thirty years past the robbery of the Okaga, and there is a blight affecting apples on Ben-Two. The cooperative controlling the orchards has little faith that a Confederation patrol ship is going to come, and even if they do, they won't know about the existence of the blight. The cooperative is in radio communication with the Okaga, still hung in orbit of Ben-Three, and the cooperative is trying to convince them to bring seeds out of storage. The Okaga is resistant, because they are concerned plants from those seeds will be blighted, too.


### <span class="org-todo todo PLAN">PLAN</span> Act 3 {#act-3}

Act 3, it’s a long while after the sabotage, the Okaga has been dismantled and its parts and crew integrated into the system’s own culture. However, without support from the galactic community, there are large blights affecting primary crops, and other issues caused by basically homogeneity and monocultures. The society resolves this by applying the confederacy’s means of organization on a smaller level, encouraging cultivation of local crops and other decentralized research into themselves.


### <span class="org-todo todo PLAN">PLAN</span> Conclusion {#conclusion}

n the conclusion, a patrol ship finally pulls into the system, and is amazed to find a society that is doing really well, relative to others. It brings up questions about whether the Confederation is really doing good work by propping up these systems, if it needs to change its methods, etc. It looks like the government of the confederation is willing to change itself, immediately, based on these observed benefits. At the same time, there is a heavy interest in exploiting this system for its new crops and appropriating their innovations for the benefit of the Confederacy, as-is. The story ends on these contrasting tones.
