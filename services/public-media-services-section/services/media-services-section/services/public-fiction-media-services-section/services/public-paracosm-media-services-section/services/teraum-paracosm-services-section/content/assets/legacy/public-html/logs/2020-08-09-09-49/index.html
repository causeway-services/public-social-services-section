Tag: Teraum.

I was talking with a friend and was saying that I think I would like to basically restart Teraum with a world generated by available online tools, so that it has a sensible climate and so on.

The central conceit of Teraum has always been, in some phrasing, “Earth, but with magic that's a physical thing.” The notion of a planet where magic builds up in certain organs of certain plants and animals has an appeal to me: it allows a rather reasonable presence of magic.

In Teraum-that-was, the world was artificial and the magic leaked in from beneath, and fell from the sky. In Teraum-that-is, I think I'd like to have it be an astronomical phenomenon: some galactic eddy casts off magic, that falls through Teraum's atmosphere to accrue below.

What effect would this accrual of magic have? I was ruminating on this last night, and I think it'd be interesting for magic to basically be, underneath itself, “wish-magic.” A dandelion who can feel the sun's warmth just inches from its own shade might wish to move over there. What if it could make that wish come true?

It might build up magic in a root nodule, and expend it to rapidly bud itself into the new area. Or its seed puffs might net themselves together, forming a balloon, and the whole plant uproots itself on a breeze and settles back down into its fresh spot.

I think this is an interesting enough premise on its own, because of difference in desires between different life-forms. There would, I suppose, be an evolution of protective mechanisms: grass about to be crushed by a landing dandelion-balloon might develop spikes which lacerate the dandelion's roots, preventing a safe landing.

But then the grass would have an injured dandelion crashed on itself, anyway. Another mechanism would probably be more fit.

I get here to the problem of this premise, and well, the problem at any fantasy world that tries to “solve” itself, in a sense: there is so much to reason through to justify it.

It makes me wonder if maybe it should just be a fictional world, no real magic or fantastic elements to speak of at all: just, a fictional world to play around in. I can still have fun making up new plants and animals, and if they're anything too weird, I just would need to make sure they live in a very small area, so that any “what about X consequence of this lifeform?” questions that get asked and answered don't have too far of consequences.

Of course if it's a world without magic, the name “Teraum,” which is a portmanteau of “Terra with thaums,” borrowing Terry Pratchett's word for the quantum particle for magic, doesn't make much sense.

There's a lot of pressure in naming a fantasy world, so I'm not going to stress too much about it right now.

So, to sum up: no magic, realistic climate, just a fictional world to play around in. Which should still be plenty fun, I think!

What's funny is, since this is mostly, right now, being developed to be the basis for a MUD, is that I'm basically planning to make this whole fake world… and my only plans for gameplay are letting folk pick various herbs to make into tea.* Tasks
