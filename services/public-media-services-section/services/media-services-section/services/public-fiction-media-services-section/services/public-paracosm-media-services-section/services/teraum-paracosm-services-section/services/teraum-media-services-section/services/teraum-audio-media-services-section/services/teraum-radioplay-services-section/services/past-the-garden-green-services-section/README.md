# Past the Garden Green

Don't go past the garden green  
Don't go past the garden green  
Bring in the chickens  
Wash all the dishes  
But don't go past the garden green

*Past the Garden Green* is a series of scripts about the children of
several families who all live in a shared estate in the Green Delta.
Their parents all leave the estate to work in the nearby town, locking
the heavy gates behind them when they leave in the morning, and with
firm instructions
