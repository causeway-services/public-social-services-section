* The Crown of Idbol Bark
The town of Belcaer used to be a sleepy place that travelers going
from the Green Delta to Unseen Sea would visit. But over the past
several months, goblins have been kidnapping travelers (and eating
their horses.) Recently, the goblins have even raided the farms on the
outskirts of Belcaer, so the villagers have banded together to hire
anyone able to find the goblins and eliminate them.

The goblins are staying in a 80' tall, 30' diameter, roughly circular
limestone tower southeast of town, after migrating there from the
Widewoods to the southwest. This is far from the main trade roads, in
an area where there are several chert outcroppings that break the
fields, making it hard to maintain bearing or keep a far watch.

[how does theparty learn about the tower?]

The tower's entrance has been sealed with the same limestone bricks as
the tower - but there are a couple of windows near the top, and there
are pine planks shoved into the mortar in a rough ladder up to the
windows. (Placing more than 120lb of weight on any single plank risks
breaking it, getting riskier for every 10lb.)

There are goblins working around the tower, digging toilets and
tearing up grass and stuffing it in sacks. If any goblin notices the
party, they'll inform the other goblins working outside who will work
together to try and harass the party into leaving - jeering and
throwing stones at them, hiding behind outcroppings. If the party
doesn't leave and approaches the tower, the goblins will rush toward
them, but instead climb the planks and disappear into the tower,
warning the others in their group. A couple will stay at the top of
the tower, and drop pebbles and dried grass down on the party if they
try and climb into the tower.

In the evening, the owlbear mom that nests on top of the tower will
also harass the party, with the intention of picking up anything
edible that's small enough (<120lb, so may attempt to carry small
party members).

If the party gets to the top of the tower, #1,  (not sure how, lol) they'd
find 2 owlbear hatchlings, that they could take and train if they knew
how - and killed the mom.

When the party enters the room at the top of the tower, through the
window, #2, they'll find an empty stone room with some sticks, dry
piles of dung, buckets, and a large brass dish that's clearly been
used for fires. The goblins will retreat down underneath the trap
door, #4.

There are three other "floors" inside the tower, though they are empty
except for more dirty straw and dung, #3. There is a large, heavy wood
door in the floor at the ground level, #4.

Once the players are inside the tower and the goblins have retreated,
they will descend to the ground floor.

The trap door has no stairs under it - there is a ladder the goblins
use, but they removed it as they retreated from the party. The party
will be unable to see the ladder before they dropped the ~7 feet into
the first level of the tower's basement, #5.

The room is circular, a bit wider in diameter than the tower above,
and is full of farm tools and preserved foodstuff and grains.
