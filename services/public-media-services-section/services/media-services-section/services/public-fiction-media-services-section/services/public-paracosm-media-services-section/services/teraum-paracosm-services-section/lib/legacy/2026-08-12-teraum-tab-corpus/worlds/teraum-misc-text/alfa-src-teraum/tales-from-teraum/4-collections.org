* /Tales from Teraum/, #4: "Collections"
"This little beauty is from before the war," Barnabus said, holding
the slick metal cube close to the lantern.

"Er, which war is that then?" Jenos asked. Barnabus thought for a
moment before responding, "All of them, I suppose."

"What's it do?" Jenos asked. Barnabus leaned back in his chair,
reaching blindly toward the cluttered shelf behind him. After a
moment, he pulled down a cup of tea which he'd left there in earlier
in the evening. Jenos watched patiently as Barnabus dropped the cube
in the mug.
