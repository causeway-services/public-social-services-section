\section*{Overview}
\label{sec:org753278f}
\textbf{\emph{Bellybrush}} is town in the \emph{Green Delta}. It is the primary suburb of
the human city of \emph{Ack}, located about a half-mile east along the
\emph{Kingsroad}. The town was founded in 701bc as a retirement community for
the fisherfolk of \emph{Ack}, and quickly grew into an agricultural community
in its own right, focused around the raising of \emph{pigs}.

\subsection*{Bellybrush During the Lull}
\label{sec:org304ee55}
\index{22pc!"Bellybrush During the Lull" in /Bellybrush Gazetteer/}
\index{34pc!"Bellybrush During the Lull" in /Bellybrush Gazetteer/}
\index{53pc!"Bellybrush During the Lull" in /Bellybrush Gazetteer/}
The \emph{Lull} was the brief period of time after the \emph{Age of Heroes}, but
before the start of the \emph{Great Wars}, roughly 20pc to 40pc. It was a
peaceful and prosperous time for Bellybrush, which had served as a
base for many of the heroes of the previous age.

Those heroes, now retired, helped \emph{humans} begin to take their first
steps toward recovering from the \emph{Collapse} here in town. For example,
in 22pc, the \emph{Chandlers Guild} office, at the time the organization's
headquarters, was where the \emph{wax clock} was invented.

In 34pc, the first issue of the newspaper \emph{The Bellybrush Review} was
printed.

In 53pc, \emph{Barnabus Trent} moved to town and established \emph{Ye Olde Curio
shop}.

\subsubsection*{Gibbet Street}
\label{sec:orgfe16cc8}
\index{Gibbet Street!"Gibbet Street" in /Bellybrush Gazetteer/}
\textbf{\emph{Gibbet Street}} is one of the oldest streets in the town, and one of
the poorest. At the southern end of the street, at the intersection
with \emph{Boundary Street} there is a barren gibbet: two tall poles on
either side of the street, with a thick board slung between them. The
poles are covered in \emph{death masks}, a mundane treasure used to remember
those executed for their crimes.

Near where Gibbet Street intersects with the \emph{Walk}, there is an
assemblage of old wooden houses that have grown into one property.
Among the residents are \emph{Wadren Smith}, a 16-year-old orphan who raises pigs,
and \emph{Holman Holder}, a 55-year old who "steals" those pigs, as a prank.
\emph{Nell} also spends a lot of time here, even though she lives at the
orphanage.

\index{Squeal, Madrin!"Madrin" in /Bellybrush Gazetteer/}

great-great-great-grandfather was a \emph{goblin} named \emph{Madrin Squeal}.
Madrin was born in 44pc, in the town of \emph{Marby}, southwest of
Bellybrush.

An emotional child, Madrin had trouble growing up and finding work as
an adult. He found himself in the custody of the \emph{asylum} by the time
he was in his twenties. He was held there until the 28th of Winter,
80pc, when he escaped.

After his escape, he built himself a shack in the area of \emph{Gibbet
Street}. His emotional outbursts have recently been getting worse, and
he's begun to develop a strange body odor, smelling strongly of hay.
