**** DRAFT Jerry's Hallway
     :PROPERTIES:
     :CUSTOM_ID: jerrys-hallway
     :END:
*/Jerry's Hallway/* is a part of the Worldkeep where
Jerry the Nowit is trapped. It is a
concrete tunnel which loops back on itself. The hallway
has no entrance or exit. The hallway was originally part of a dwarven
facility which experimented with magic - the tunnel was
used to accelerate and collide magic particles, to try and find more
basic components.

Jerry has been trapped in the hallway since the
Collapse: he was pulled into his copy of Gnork's /Encyclopedia of Dwarven Construction/.

****** Creation
       :PROPERTIES:
       :CUSTOM_ID: worldkeep-history-creation
       :END:
 Created by the gods in 54000bc, the facility was conceived as a
 storage facility for their excess creations, notably those which
 proved incompatible with the rest of their work.
****** Breach
       :PROPERTIES:
       :CUSTOM_ID: worldkeep-history-breach
       :END:
 In 30000bc, the Worldkeep's walls breached, allowing some species to
 escape. The first species to escape were giant spiders into the
 Widewoods, followed by goblins. Orcs were able to move into the
 Farsteppes, while humans moved into the Central Plains. Dwarves built
 fortresses in the Worldkeepers. In the New World, halflings and elves
 were able to make a home for themselves.
