## Premise

The party is hired to guard *Ye Olde Curio Shop* — Bellybrush's thrift
store / curio dealer — overnight. Something has been making sounds in
the shop at night.

**Adventure question:** What's making sounds at night?

Set in the year 80pc, during the Reconstruction Era.
