lore: "About 54,000 years ago, the gods from across different universes got together because they were having issues managing all the various creatures and plants they'd created — elves, giant spiders, sentient poisonous vines, that sort of thing. They came up with a plan to store the excess in what would also be a zoo, so they could show off their work. They built the Worldkeep. Because it was rather industrial-looking — full of corridors and habitats — they built a proper planet around it as a park, with oceans and continents, pine trees and salmon. That world is Teraum. The gods stopped visiting; the planet outlived its purpose."
