* DRAFT Introduction
{{{document-isdraft}}}

The */Catalog of Mundane Things/* is a directory of common objects and
stuff found in the fantasy world of Teraum. This book is intended as a
resource for getting a deeper feel for the setting, and doesn't have
much use on its own.

the /Widewoods/.
** DRAFT Cokeberries
   :PROPERTIES:
   :CUSTOM_ID: cokeberries
   :END:
#+INDEX: cokeberries!"Cokeberries" in /Catalog of Mundane Things/
*/Cokeberries/* are a tart purple berry that grows natively in
/Arathel County/ and have been cultivated around /Gnalens/, in the
/New World/. They are used to make /wine/, /preserves/, and /dye/, among
other cottage uses.
** DRAFT Death Masks
   :PROPERTIES:
   :CUSTOM_ID: death-masks
   :END:
#+INDEX: masks, death!"Death Masks" in /Catalog of Mundane Things/
*/Death masks/* are created in the town of /Bellybrush/, at /Masked
Mortuary/ by /Daxol Nabrish/ and his son /Arandul/. The masks are
created using a wax cast of the deceased's face, artfully distorted
while warm, to create a clay mold.
