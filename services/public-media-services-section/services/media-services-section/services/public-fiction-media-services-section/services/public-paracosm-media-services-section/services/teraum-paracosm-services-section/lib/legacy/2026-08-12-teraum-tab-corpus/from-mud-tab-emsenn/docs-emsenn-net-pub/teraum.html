Teraum

Here's some pages to learn about Teraum. Unless otherwise stated, assume information about Teraum is about the world as it was about 80 years after the Teraumian Collapse, and information is, unless otherwise specified, presented how the Red Union does (or would) talk about it.

Atlas-at-a-Glance

Two major known continents: Eastland and Westland, separated by the Optic Ocean

Eastland has:

  • Central Plains
    • Plains, sandhills, etc.
  • Farsteppes
    • Steppes
    • Trading town of Helmet's Dent
  • Farfarsteppes
    • (Usually frozen) steppes
  • Green Delta
    • Marshes and farmland
    • Big city of Ack and its suburb Bellybrush
    • Four counties: Arathel, Marby, Pled, Sherwyn
  • Unseen Sea
    • Vast desert
    • Tayopolis
  • Widewoods
  • Worldkeepers

Westland has:

  • Elflands
    • Dense temperate forests
  • Halfling Country
    • Rolling hills and farmland
    • Gnalens
    • Westland Coast
      • Trepi Island

Factions

  • House Oru
    • Self-declared sovereignty in the Farsteppes, led by Renkah Oru and her son Kolpe Oru acting as governor.
    • The Red Union invoices House Ellon for the lands Oru claims, so does not recognize their soveriegnty.
  • House Ellon
    • Defunct, territory claimed by Oru. Rumored Last Baron of House Ellon
  • Blacksmith's Guild
    • Ackian guild and now Red Union guild for blacksmithing and other smithing. Headquartered in the Copper Ward of Ack.
    • Atrix Hucklebee is a member.
  • Chandler's Guild
    • candlemakers and printing press
    • Member of the Red Union
    • Headquartered in Ack, with offices in Bellybrush, Marby, and Sherwyn
    • Wybert is a member
  • Cloudbound Trading Company
    • Transportation company focused on, but not limited to, airship travel. Headquarted in Ack with offices in Bellybrush, Marby, Sherwyn, Arathel, Pled, Helmet's Dent, Gnalens, and Tayopolis.
    • member of the Red Union
    • Stevie was a member, now deceased
  • Gnalens is a city but also a faction.
    • democratic republic on the coast of Halfling Country
    • run by The Governor of Gnalens
      • has a Lieutenant Governor of Gnalens, currently Ander Falone
  • Leatherworkers Guild
    • Member of the Red Union
    • Offices in Ack and Gnalens
    • Wayne Helder is a member
  • Red Union
    • Bureaucrats who control many human systems with debt and credit
    • Originated in the Red Ward of Ack, with offices established in many former temples anyplace humans live.
