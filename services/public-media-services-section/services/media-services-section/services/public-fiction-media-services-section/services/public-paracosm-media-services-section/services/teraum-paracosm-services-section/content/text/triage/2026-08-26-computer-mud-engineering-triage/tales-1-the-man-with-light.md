## Plot beats (from a 2025-08 sketch)

- Sendvogue invents the lumorb (the lightbulb).
- The Chandlers Guild tries to repress it (their candle and wax-wand
  business is threatened by reliable artificial light).
- Sendvogue uses the recently-invented printing press (not yet
  moveable-type) to make posters sharing the schematics — making
  suppression impossible.
