This document is a list of the people who have had a direct hand in
helping me craft Teraum. It is, unfortunately, incomplete, as there
were many people who helped in ways I no longer recall. If you
remember contributing, and aren't listed here, /please/ let me know!
#+INCLUDE: "../plates.inc::default-eli"
* Credits
  :PROPERTIES:
  :CUSTOM_ID: credits
  :END:
** Brinkley, Jeb
*/Jeb Brinkley/* is the person who created Jenos Anadini and played him
from the 12th to 18th of Blooming, 80pc. To help develop Jenos, Jeb
also contributed a lot to the broad strokes of Pled County and the
Great Wars.
** Childs, Lucas
*/Lucas Childs/* was a friend who helped inspire me to turn Teraum
into a fantasy canon, and came up with the name /Brave Old World/ for
the accompanying ruleset.
** Little, Stirling
*/Stirling Little/* is the person who created Renkah Oru and controlled
her from the 3rd of Showering until the 18th of Blooming, 80pc. He
also drew some of the art from that era. He maintains a presence on
Instagram at [[https://instagram.com/stirlzzz][@stirlzzz]].
** emsenn
*/emsenn/* is the original creator of /Teraum/.
