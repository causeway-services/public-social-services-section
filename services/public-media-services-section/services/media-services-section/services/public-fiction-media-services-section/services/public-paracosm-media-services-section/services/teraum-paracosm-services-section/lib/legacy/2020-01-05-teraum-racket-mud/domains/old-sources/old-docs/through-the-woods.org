* Through the Woods
The party is in a small village, and overhears a married couple
discussing how they don't want to send their daughter throughh the
woods to bring her sickly grandma some food.

The party goes through the woods to grandma's house, and upon
arriving, finds a grandma with goatlike pupils, short horns, and a
forked tongue.

How to resolve? Her symptoms get worse as she turns into a bugbear,
growing more violent.
