A designer-note being. Nadim has no body, no scene presence, no
location data — but he's referenced often enough that the project's
realism depends on his absence being legible.

When writing a Hevî Mala story, invoking Nadim is a tell that something
is being held in place: a contradiction smoothed, an unowned failure
absorbed, a missing person papered over. Use sparingly. He should never
be explained.
