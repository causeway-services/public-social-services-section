***** Biography
****** Early Life
Edward Gnork was born in the swampy town of Arathel in
122bc. He had an innate dislike of the environment and so,
at a young age, traveled to the CentralPlains. He became a trader between dwarves in the
region and humans, and eventually developed an interest in dwarven
culture, especially their architecture.
****** Architectural Studies
Edward Gnork became well-known among the dwarves he
traded with, who happily explained how they built things. From their
perspective, Gnork was an amusement, and perhaps one of the world's
first tourists.
****** Death
When exploring the ruins of the Snapstone Enclave in the
Central Plains, Edward Gnork went down a ladder
into a tunnel and while exploring the area died of carbon monoxide poisoning.
