The geography of /Arathel County/, wooded and cut with streams, makes
it easy for people to keep their privacy. One such private person was
/Lorrestar Beyn/, a /human/ who lived in the region during the /Magic
Revolution/.

In his large manor, he maintained a vast library of writing about
/magic/, until the /Collapse/ occurred, and all /magic/ disappeared
from the world.

Eight decades have passed since then. Nestled back in the woods,
/Lorrestar's manor/ sits. Locals spread rumors that it is haunted;
that a strange beast lives in the manor's attic; that no one who
visits is ever seen again.

So, there must be something good inside, right?
