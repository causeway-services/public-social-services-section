***Glasya-Labolas*** is the 25th [[Spirit of the Ars Goetia]]. He is a [[president]] and [[earl]], and [[commanding|commands]] 36 [[legion|legions]].

>“He is a Mighty President and Earl, appearing in the form of a dog with wings like a griffin. He teacheth all arts in an instant, and is the author and captain of all manslaughters and bloodshed. He teacheth all things past and to come. He causeth the love of friends and foes. He can make men invisible and is an excellent familiar. He governeth 36 legions of spirits.” - [[Ars Goetia]]



Glasya-Labolas is noted for his **capacity to reveal knowledge instantly**, including about the past and future—but also for his association with **murder, violence, betrayal**, and the **manipulation of social bonds** (both healing and rupturing them). In traditional texts, he is treated as **highly dangerous** not because of chaotic behavior, but because of his **strategic coherence in systems of deception, loyalty inversion, and lethal intent.**
