*** Alindest

*/Alindest/* is a small human village in Marby County, located on the
road to Arlonest, south of Ack. Located at marker 23 on the map of the
Green Delta.
*** Arathel
#+INDEX: Green Delta, towns!"Arathel" in /Geography and Settlements of the Green Delta/
#+INDEX: Arathel!"Arathel" in /Geography and Settlements of the Green Delta/
*/Arathel/* is the human-populated county seat of Arathel County, in
the northwest Green Delta. First settled in 478bc as a logging
community, Arathel is one of the larger towns in the community. It is
governed by a council of local commercial leaders. its primary exports
are wool, limestone, and cokeberries. Notably, Arathel was the capital
of the Astar Empire. For more information, see "Arathel" in the
/Arathel County Gazetteer/.
**** DRAFT Bellybrush
     :PROPERTIES:
     :CUSTOM_ID: bellybrush
     :END:
 */Bellybrush/* is town in the Green Delta. It is the primary suburb of
 the human city of Ack, located about a half-mile east along the
 Kingsroad. The town was founded in 701bc as a retirement community for
 the fisherfolk of Ack, and quickly grew into an agricultural community
 in its own right, focused around the raising of pigs.
