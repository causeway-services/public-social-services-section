# Hevi Mala

A paracosm centered on a clinic and relay outpost called **Hevî Mala**
(the building stencil reads **MARKAZ 9**) in the Hala Basin — a
fictionalized northeast-Syria border region modeled on post-2011
conflict collapse.

The fiction is built as a story mesh rather than a single plot line:
every story either passes through the clinic, depends on it, or feels
its effects at a distance. Characters do not grow. The world does not
advance. It holds.

- [Project frame](texts/project-frame.md) — abridged manifesto: the
  fiction project's tonal and structural commitments at a glance.
- [The Hala Basin](texts/hala-basin.md) — abridged setting overview.
- [Core characters](texts/core-characters.md) — abridged cast roster.
- [The clinic at Hevi Mala](texts/the-clinic-at-hevi-mala.md) —
  abridged site description.
- [Triage synthesis](texts/triage-synthesis.md) — a source-witness
  synthesis covering material the abridged texts above don't carry on
  their own: the ethical framework, story/play/contribution guidance
  (narrative rhythms, the four-part modulation loop, story-activation
  prompts, contribution length/naming conventions, continuity
  protocols), an earlier naming layer for the basin's geography (Sarzam
  / Sarzami, Anya Bari, Chem Zewi, Chort, Badfields, Halan Backroads,
  Zinar Swamp), and a witness-by-witness audit of every prior draft.
- [Praxonic overlay](texts/praxonic-overlay.md) — a synthetic-vocabulary
  reframing of Hevi Mala ("the recursive emission of care under
  collapse"). Explicitly bracketed off from the canonical project by
  its own source material; kept as an inactive interpretive layer, not
  active canon.

## Provenance

This directory consolidates the Hevi Mala material scattered across
several `archive.emsenn.net` triage snapshots (multiple mirrored copies
of a March-2026 intake pass, plus an even earlier RPG-domain
publication and a `wsl-backup` combined-notes dump). The March-2026
`hevi-mala-triage-synthesis.md` (now `texts/triage-synthesis.md`)
already ran a witness-by-witness audit of every duplicate draft in that
scatter and found the remainder — `hevi-mala-storyworld-guide.md`, a
35KB RPG-domain export of the same guide, the "Sloppy Hala Basin
Worlding Guide" family, and several `old-notes`/`relationality-triage`/
`applications` copies — added nothing beyond formatting and path
history once absorbed into the synthesis; those are not duplicated
here. The one exception is `praxonic-overlay.md`, kept as the original
compositional artifact rather than only its prose paraphrase in the
synthesis.
