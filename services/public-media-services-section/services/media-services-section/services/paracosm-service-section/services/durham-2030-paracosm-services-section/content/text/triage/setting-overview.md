***Durham 2030*** is a speculative fiction setting representing an
imagining of Durham in the year 2030. Written in winter 2023, the
fiction assumes that TESCREAL ideology — transhumanism, Extropianism,
singularitarianism, cosmism, Rationalism, Effective Altruism,
longtermism — has reshaped local settlements into technofascist
enclaves known as **arcologies**.

Within the arcologies, residents are governed by a Social Contribution
Score regime: a centrally-monitored metric that gates access to
resources and conditions every aspect of daily life. The fiction
explores what civic life, work, and resistance look like inside this
configuration of Durham — and what happens at the edges, where the
arcology's reach thins.
