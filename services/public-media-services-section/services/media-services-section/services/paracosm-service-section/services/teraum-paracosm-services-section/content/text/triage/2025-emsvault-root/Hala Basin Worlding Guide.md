This [[text]] [[defining|defines]] the [[fictional world]] of [[Hevi Mala]], a [[medical clinic]] located in the [[Hala Basin]] region within the territory of [[Sarzam]] during a period of ongoing [[militarized conflict]].

The world is [[designing|designed]] a [[fiction setting]] and [[tabletop role-playing game setting]].

## Themes

The world of the Hala Basin exists for one function: to recursively interrogate the following line-of-inquiry:
- How does what stories we believe shape what we see?
- How does what we see shape what we feel?
- How does what we feel shape what we know?
- How does what we know shape what stories we believe?

In the Hala Basin, time is slow enough that beliefs can fight over the truth before reality determines it, and folk who live in the region understand this, whether or not they know it.

Most people are trying to align the material of the present with the temporality the future so that it proves them correct: picking the long route to the city so they are right to have brought so much fuel; collaborating with soldiers to create a conflict zone so they have to move away; arguing over which crop to plant so long that the shorter-seasoned seed is necessary.

Things don't need to be true - they just need to be coherent enough to do what's being done.

## Locations

Most places in the Hevi Mala world have more than one name, depending on who is talking about it. The sections below use a mix of naming; assume every place has at least a Turkishand Kurmanji-influenced name, with many places also having Persian-influenced names.

### Anya Bari

A [[village]] located on the western edge of the Zinar Swamp. Was abandoned when the Zinar Canal was destroyed, creating the Swamp, but resettled after the floodwaters retreated. Well-connected to Hasur via the dried Canal, but free of much of the conflict. Felt as a suburban paradise compared to the Basin or Hasur, but environmental contamination provides significant hardship.

### Chem Zewi

The [[Chem Zewi]] is a [[river]] that provides the Hala Basin with its southern border. Similar to the [[Euphrates River]]. Name derives from [[Kurmanji]] for "olive river." There are small villages here, but they struggle due to pollution in the river.

### Chort

The [[Chort]] is a [[border wall]] constructed by the country to the north. Similar to the [[Turkey-Syria border]]. Name derives from [[Kurdish slang]] for [[liar]].

### Badfields

The [[Badfields]] are located in the western part of the Hala Basin. Littered with abandoned oil extraction equipment, the terrain here is scarred with fissures and frequently experiences small quakes, opening up new pits.

### Hala Basin

The Hala Basin is the [[region]] where almost everything in the stories happens. Bordered on the south by Chem Zewi and on the north by the Chort. Geographically similar to [[Earth]]'s [[Palmyride/Sinjar Trough]].

### Halan Backroads

The [[Halan Backroads]] are the only way around the Hala Basin, since the [[Sarzami highways]] were destroyed early in the [[Sarzami Conflict]]. The roads are inconsistent. Asphalt turns to gravel turns to ruts turns to impressions turns to implication turns to inference turns to guess.

### Hasur

Similar to [[Raqqa]]. Roughly two hours from Hevi Mala by car on the Backroads. The Zinar Canal was going to connect to it, but was never completed. In Hasur, checkpoint locations are consistent, factional control isn't. 

### Hevi Mala

Located somewhere near the middle of the Hala Basin. Was originally a farming village, became what it was due to geographical proximity and distance.

### Maqam al-Nahr

Maqam al-Nahr is a small hilltop shrine, located toward the west edge of the Hala Basin, where hills build up against the banks of the Chem Zewi.

### Sarzam

Similar to [[Syria]]. Name derives from [[Persian language|Persian]] for "[[land]]," *sarzarim*.

### Sifir Station

A prominent [[military outpost]] located along the [[Chort]]. Name derives from Kurmanji for [[zero]].

### Zinar Swamp

The Zinar Swamp is an accidental artificial wetland that was created when the Zinar Canal's connection to the Chem Zewi was destroyed during the Sarzam Conflict. In places, anti-air cannon turrets rise from the mire like rotten tree stumps. The water here is polluted with dissolved chemical weapons. The flow of water between the Swamp and Chem pollutes the river.
