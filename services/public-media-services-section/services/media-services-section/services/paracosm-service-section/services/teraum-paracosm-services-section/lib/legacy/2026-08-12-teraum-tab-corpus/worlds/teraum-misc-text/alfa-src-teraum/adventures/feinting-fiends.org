*/Feinting Fiends/* is an adventure for creative players with
characters of any skill level, intended for play with the /Brave Old
World/ ruleset. It invites the characters to investigate suspicious
noises occurring in an old man's curio shop. It is set on Teraum, a
tragically funny fantasy setting, during the early days of the
Reconstruction Era, when humanity is recovering from the apocalyptic
disappearance of magic 80 years prior.

/Note:/ Feinting Fiends /makes several references to/ The Old
Curiosity Shop /by Charles Dickens./
