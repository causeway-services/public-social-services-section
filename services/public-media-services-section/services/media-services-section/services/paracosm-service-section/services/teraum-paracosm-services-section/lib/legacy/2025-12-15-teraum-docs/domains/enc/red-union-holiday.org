#+title: Red Union holiday
#+date: [2025-09-06 Sat 09:16]

A */Red Union holiday/* is one of five (sometimes six) [[id:ab5142cf-d7d3-4435-b6eb-d938c606fece][days]] in the [[id:8e882eb5-22d5-4dc6-b6b5-0e067efa566b][Red Union calendar]] that help its 30-day [[id:de5b0001-2975-43d5-bfad-a408e1b43e39][month]] structure line up with [[id:7f527251-b5fc-4546-9fc7-8b6af33451e0][Teraum]]'s 365.25-day [[id:b6e247c8-7e74-46b4-8ed4-e25d2335dd2c][year]].

They're called holidays, holy-days, because the Red Union is salty about the divine origins of their environment and the many ways it complicates their systems, like a clean 30-day, 12-month calendar.
