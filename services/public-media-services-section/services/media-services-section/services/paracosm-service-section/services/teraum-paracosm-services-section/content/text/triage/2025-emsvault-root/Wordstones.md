footsteps on cobbles tapping hard  
like fast moving fingers  
exploding typewriter tape  
stories told then hit the streets  
  
shoes grinding up your grandfather's tale  
off comes a piece and scattered it far  
grains of stories all spread around  
its the way legends happen  
  
words washed by winds  
into the curb flowing down  
mix with the bedrock  
tumble downstream  
  
words flow down to ocean waves  
sinking treasures bubbling downstream  
washed ashore on a stormy day  
mingle with beaches and the shells  
  
up comes a girl with shovel and pail  
scoops up some sand here comes our tale  
press it down and there's a tower  
this story of a story in a grain of sand
