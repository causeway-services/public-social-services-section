summary: "A physical substance leaking from the Worldkeep; pressurized as fuel during the Magic Revolution; absent since the Collapse."
lore: |
  Magic is a physical substance — not an energy or force, but matter. The gods imbued it into the Worldkeep when they built it. As the Worldkeep walls fractured starting ~30,000bB, magic began to leak out into the rest of Teraum, working its way through the ground "like veins of ore."

  Magic creatures (elves, dwarves, halflings, goblins, orcs, giant spiders, talking trees) have magic in their blood, sap, or equivalent. They need it to thrive. Humans are mundane — without magic in them — which made them disadvantaged early on (they couldn't hold land against magic races) but eventually advantageous (they survived the Collapse).

  Humans learned to use magic by putting it under pressure. Pressurized magic became fuel for foundries that churned out vast quantities of steel. Some humans learned to do magical things directly with magic. This was the basis of the Magic Revolution.

  The Collapse occurred because too much magic was gathered in one place — specifically, the vast quantities stored in the bank vaults under Ack — which caused all magic everywhere in the world to use itself up. The MASH-canonical version attributes the Collapse to Dakkle's siphon instead; both explanations exist in canon.
