* The Celson Estate
In the town of Helmet's Dent in the Farsteppes, a man named Nash
Celson claims that his nearby house has been taken over by bandits. In
truth, he's a bandit/con artist, looking to dupe some adventurers into
massacring the estate's rightful inhabitants, the Culver family.
