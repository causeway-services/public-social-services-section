\subsubsection*{Horsefolk Territory}
\label{sec:org36fd714}
\textbf{\emph{Horsefolk Territory}} is the large region of the \emph{Farsteppes} - most
of it - that is still unclaimed and uncharted by the \emph{Red Union} and
affiliated organizations.

While the \emph{horsefolk} are largely nomadic, there are many small
settlements scattered throughout the region.
\subsection*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Settlements}
\label{sec:orgf718538}
Most settlements in the region are collections of dung and thatch
huts. It is tradition in the region to paint the houses with the local
red clay, in a game. The owner of the hut dips their hand in clay
paint and strikes their hand against the wall. Others in the community
call out animals and shapes, and owner attempts to paint them.
\subsubsection*{Helmet's Dent}
\label{sec:org00bf19d}
\index{Helmet's Dent!"Helmet's Dent" in /Geography and Settlements of the Farsteppes/}
\textbf{\emph{Helmet's Dent}} is a \emph{human} town in the Farsteppes. Originally a
mining community, it has become a trading community that facilitates
commerce between the nomadic \emph{human} tribes of the Farsteppes and the
settled \emph{humans} in the \emph{Central Plains}.

\label{sec:org4fe4074}
\index{House Ellon!"House Ellon" in /Geography and Settlements of the Farsteppes/}
\textbf{\emph{House Ellon}} is a defunct noble house in the Farsteppes that
previously controlled what is now \emph{Castle Oru} and the lands around
