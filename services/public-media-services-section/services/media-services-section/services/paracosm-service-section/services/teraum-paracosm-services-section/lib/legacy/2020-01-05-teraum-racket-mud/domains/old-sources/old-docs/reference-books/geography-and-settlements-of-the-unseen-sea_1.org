** DRAFT Overview
#+BEGIN_QUOTE
When human pilgrims first got to the Coast of the Unseen Sea, it was
after slogging through the muck of the Gloaming for weeks. They were
tired, and hungry. So it's understandable how they mistook the Unseen
Sea for an ocean. An endless desert that rolls out into the horizon,
at the base of a miles-long cliff? With heat waves rolling off it, I
could see how it might seen like a very clear body of water, until you
reach the bottom and realize the illusion.

But what I don't understand is why they choose to stick with the name.
It makes us all look a bit daft.
#+END_QUOTE

The */Unseen Sea/* is a region past decline. Once the playground of
wizards and other magical beasts, who took full advantage of the miles
of dunes and salt flats, the region is considered dying. Almost all
the humans have moved away or perished, and only the once-vibrant city
of /Tayopopopolous/ remains, sustained by airship trade that comes in
from the /Green Delta/.
