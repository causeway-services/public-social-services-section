(Plot sketch only — the full Tale was never written.)

Set during the early years of bureaucratization. Kettel, an 11-year-old
girl, lives with her two brothers (one a year older, one two years
younger) in a town. A four-year-older sister is away working to pay
Union debt.

Kettel is quizzed for placement in a Homeschool — a boarding school —
and passes. She likes her friends and living in town, doesn't want to
leave, and tries to resist, criticizing the Homeschool program. Her
reasoning is dismissed as childish and naive, not congruent with "adult
logic" — i.e., bureaucratic Union logic.

(Reference to ideas of late liberalism, where families and communities
enforce the logic of the state, regardless of state violence.)
