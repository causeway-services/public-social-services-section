** Description 
    
  #+name: outside-description
  The */Crossed Candles Inn/* is a relatively simple building: four posts are sunk deep into the silty clay soil of [[file:arathel_county.org][Arathel County]], some crossbeams were braced into place, and vertical slats were hammered on over those. Some windows are covered with shutters, and an open doorway faces toward a fork in the [[file:arathel_ack_road.org][Arathel-Ack road]].

  #+name: orientation-description
  From outside the */Crossed Candles Inn/*, the [[file:arathel_ack_road.org][Arathel-Ack road]] continues *north* toward [[file:arathel_county.org][Arathel County]], *southeast* toward the town of [[file:bellybrush.org][Bellybrush]], and *southwest* toward [[file:ack.org][Ack]].

  
  #+name: inside-description
  Inside, the */Crossed Candles Inn/* is a simple wood shack, with several shuttered windows. There are a few wood cots in one corner, and a long table which serves as the bar in another. During the day, there are usually a few travelers sitting at the bar, resting as they move across the [[file:game.org][GAME]].

** Secrets                                                         :noexport:

   - There is a ghost here named */Alfric/*. Alfric died after slipping and hitting his head on the bar while trying to start a bar fight, after surviving many dangerous battles in the [[file:great_wars.org][Great Wars]].
   - The wood beams that provide the building's frame have been lightly fired, something done throughout [[file:arathel_county.org][Arathel County]] to protect against water damage.
