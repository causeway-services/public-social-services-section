*/Brave Old World/* is a [[id:884d994c-be55-4ca7-a728-2af6a57f8c38][tabletop role-playing game ruleset]], based on 
[[id:eb1d06db-27f3-4f40-9c77-8a9fa07512bf][Apocalypse World]]. It was built for playing [[id:893f3eeb-a075-4cc6-991b-531be087d8a6][tabletop role-playing games]] set in [[id:7f527251-b5fc-4546-9fc7-8b6af33451e0][Teraum]].


* Brave Old World Podcast

Brave Old World was also the name of a [[id:47d513fe-99ed-41ab-acb0-c577679f0037][podcast]]that I hosted where we played [[id:505b8c54-2d8d-4dc8-8ef4-2a3c43e3e026][Dungeons & Dragons]]. I was the [[id:366d2365-48ea-43ef-be50-f67d89e9320e][dungeon master]] for a [[id:ffa71335-e36f-4c25-898b-56f1c9bc030a][Dungeon & Dragons campaign]] where two groups of players explored Teraum
