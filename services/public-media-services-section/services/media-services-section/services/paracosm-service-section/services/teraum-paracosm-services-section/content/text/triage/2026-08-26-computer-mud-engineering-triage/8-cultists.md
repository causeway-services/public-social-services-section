* Tales from Teraum, #7: "Cultists"
Simon tapped once on the door, quietly counted to two, and tapped
twice, then kicked the frame of the heavy wooden door. A muffled voice
said something from the other side of the door.

"What was that?" Simon asked. "Can you speak up?"

"I said," an annoyed but quite clear voice on the other of the door
yelled, "that you need to tell me how many turnips grew in the Ackian
Plains last year."

"More than seven but less than 54,938," Simon replied after a moment's
thoughts. After an uncomfortably long period the door creaked open
and Simon slipped inside. Immediately behind him the door swung shut
under its own weight, and Simon was left
