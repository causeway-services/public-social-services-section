You stand at the eastern gate of Ack. The city walls rise above you, old stone darkened by centuries of coal smoke. Through the archway you can see the rooftops of the Tin Ward, where the sound of hammering never stops. Behind you, the Kingsroad unrolls east through farmland toward Bellybrush.

A guard leans against the gatehouse. He looks bored.
