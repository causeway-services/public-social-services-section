 The */Collapse/* was a catastrophic event in which all magic in Teraum
 destructed with chaotic effects, occuring on the 1st of Sleeting, 0pc.

 The Collapse was caused due to magic's, well, magical properties. In
 this instance, it was that "too much magic in one spot causes it all
 to destruct chaotically."
