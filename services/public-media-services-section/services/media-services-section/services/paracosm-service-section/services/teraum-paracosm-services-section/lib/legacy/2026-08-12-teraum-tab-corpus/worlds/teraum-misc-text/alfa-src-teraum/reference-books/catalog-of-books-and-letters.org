*/Gnork's Encyclopedia of Dwarven Construction/* is a manual written
by Edward Gnork in 81bc. The book contains
a breadth and depth of information about dwarven building
methods, including their recipes for concrete and schematics for
building foundries and other facilities.


***** Known Copies
 As the book was published before the advent of the printing press,
 there were very few original copies of the text, and even fewer
 survive today. It is suspected that inside the library at Honeyfern
 Laboratories]] there may be a copy. It's known that Jenos Anadini
 possess a copy.
