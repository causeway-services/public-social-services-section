### From Brave Old World Podcast

Teraum used to be a land of **magic** and **wonder**. Humanity was entering a **golden age** after discovering a New World populated with elves and halflings. Their wars with the dwarves and orcs of the Old World ended. 82 years ago, magic **vanished** in what would be called the **Collapse**. The dwarves disappeared and the elves became feral. Humanity was **alone** and cascaded into **war**. War and **wealth** created the spark of an **industrial** revolution. Industrialization brought **bureaucracy** and a new **peace**, held by debt and credit.
