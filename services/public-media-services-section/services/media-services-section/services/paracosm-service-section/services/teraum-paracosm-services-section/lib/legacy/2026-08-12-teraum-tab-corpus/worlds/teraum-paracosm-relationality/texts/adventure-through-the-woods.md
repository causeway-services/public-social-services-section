## Plot

The party is in a small village, and overhears a married couple
discussing how they don't want to send their daughter through the
woods to bring her sickly grandma some food.

The party goes through the woods to grandma's house, and upon arriving,
finds a grandma with goatlike pupils, short horns, and a forked tongue.

How to resolve? Her symptoms get worse as she turns into a bugbear,
growing more violent.

**Adventure question:** What is grandma's sickness?

Set in the year 80pc, during the Reconstruction Era.
