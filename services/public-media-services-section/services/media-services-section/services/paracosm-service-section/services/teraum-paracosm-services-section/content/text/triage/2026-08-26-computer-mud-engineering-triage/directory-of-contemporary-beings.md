 */Jerry the Nowit/* is a nowit who exists trapped in Jerry's Hallway,
 within the Worldkeep. Jerry became trapped in the Hallway during the
 Collapse]], after being pulled into his copy of Gnork's /Encyclopedia
 of Dwarven Construction/.

 Since the Collapse, Jerry has been undisturbed in his Hallway, save
 for a singular incident on the 12th of Blooming, 80pc. Then, four
 humans were teleported into the hallway during the crash of the airship /Hunnul/.
 a copy of Gnork's Encyclopedia, but the four humans, including Jenos,
 teleported away before Jerry could take it.
**** DRAFT Lystra

 worshippers on Teraum, but is quite powerful anyway.
 Notably, there is a monastery to her in the
 Elflands.
