This is a high-level chronology of Teraum, organized by Age and Era. Each
entry summarizes events that have full entries in `time/`. Use this
document as a navigational spine; use individual `time/` files for the
canonical event records.

## Ancient Age

The Ancient Age is the period between when the Worldkeep was installed
and when it fragmented. Its history focuses on what was happening inside
the Worldkeep, as the facility started to encounter issues.

- **54,000bB** — Powerful gods plant the Worldkeep deep in Teraum and
  raise the Worldkeepers around them to protect it.

## Mythic Age

The Mythic Age is the period between when the Worldkeep fractured and
when most of the strangest creatures died.

- **30,000bB** — The Worldkeep fragments.

## Imperial Age

### Classical Era

- **3,400bB** — Farsteppes: a band of humans discovers fermenting mares'
  milk. Over the next few centuries they develop the skill and become
  known as the Lazy Mare Tribe.
- **1,200bB** — Central Plains: dwarves move into the Central Plains
  from the Rollings. Green Delta: humans construct a Chauntean monastery.

### Early Imperial Era

- **490bB** — Central Plains: humans form the Indenon Empire in the
  southwestern Central Plains.
- **480bB** — Central Plains: Hill Dwarves and the Indenon Empire go to
  war.
- **470bB** — Green Delta: humans form the Astar Empire in the northern
  half of the Green Delta.
- **468bB** — Central Plains: Astar Empire attacks Indenon Empire for
  territory.
- **439bB** — Green Delta: the Rolense Empire forms in the southern half
  of the Green Delta.
- **432bB** — Green Delta: Rolense Empire attacks the Astar Empire for
  control over the Green River Delta.

## Magic Age

### Imperial Era

- **422bB, 19th of Winter** — Central Plains: Lorrestar, a human wizard
  living in the Astar Empire, sabotages the city of Indenon by causing
  all metal to melt at the same time. One of the first major uses of
  magic since the Mythic Age.
- **422bB, Midsummer** — Lorrestar is granted Astarian nobility after
  successfully destroying Indenon.
- **414bB** — Central Plains: Astar and Indenon make peace.
- **412bB** — Green Delta: Astar and Rolense make peace, with Rolense in
  control of the Green River Delta.
- **401bB** — Green Delta: Lorrestar is rewarded by the Astar with land
  to the northeast of the Green River Delta.
- **368bB** — Central Plains: Rolense is sacked by goblins from the
  Widewoods.
- **323bB** — The last of the dwarves move out of the Central Plains,
  back to the Worldkeepers.
- **287bB** — Lorrestar builds his estate in the Green Delta.
- **250bB** — Green Delta: the city of Ack begins to grow. Known for its
  smoked fish; retiring sea-captains lean their decommissioned vessels
  against the walls surrounding the town.
- **182bB** — Green Delta: Lorrestar writes and publishes *A Series of
  Unfortunate Spells*.
- **160bB** — Green Delta: the Red Ward is constructed in Ack from the
  red clay of the Green Delta — the first Ward on the north side of the
  river.
- **72bB** — Humans discover the Eastland, calling it the New World.
- **70bB** — Humans exploring the Eastlands encounter halflings.
- **69bB** — Humans exploring the Eastlands encounter elves (*Homo
  silvestris teraumensis*).
