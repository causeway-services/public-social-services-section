description: "The Collapse is the in-world Teraumic name for the event when magic vanished from the world — also called the Break. Two canonical accounts: (a) Dakkle, a creature outside the planar system, siphoned the magic; (b) the vast quantities of magic stored in the bank vaults under Ack reached critical mass, causing all magic everywhere to use itself up. Either way: midnight on the 1st of Sleeting (Deepwinter), 0pc."
defines: [the-collapse, the-break]
summary: "The instant magic vanished from Teraum, midnight 1 Sleeting 0pc."
lore: "Two canonical accounts coexist. The MASH-current version: Dakkle, a creature outside Teraum's planar system, completed a siphon they had been running on the Brave Old World's magic. The older version (writing-teraum.org): too much magic gathered in one place — specifically the vast quantities stored in Ack's bank vaults — caused all magic everywhere to use itself up. Either way: midnight on the 1st of Sleeting, 0pc, every enchantment in Teraum unraveled simultaneously, the dwarves vanished from the Worldkeepers, the elves of the New World turned feral, and the halflings lost their sapience and were hunted to extinction within weeks. Humans, being mundane, survived."
