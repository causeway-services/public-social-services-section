---
name: "Bartleby's Curse"
description: "Bartleby's Curse is a magic condition affecting any sapient creature it is directed at, applied via the waxy wand's wax. Effects on the cursed person are minimal — they only notice that reading of any sort makes them more tired than otherwise. The main effect is external: anything the cursed person reads is 'transcribed' to paper held over the waxy wand's flame, overwriting from the beginning when the page becomes full."
summary: "Magic condition: cursed person tires when reading; their reading is transcribed via the waxy wand."
lore: "The waxy wand was created by Alfred Bartleby for the Chandlers Guild in 72bB to enable 'transmission' of written information. Barnabus Trent accepted Bartleby's Curse in 51pc as a pardon condition from the Merchant Kings. The mechanism: drip wax on a target, they're cursed; anything they read appears on paper held to the wand's flame."
