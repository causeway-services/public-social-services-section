* DRAFT Encyclopedia of Magic Creatures
** DRAFT Bugbears
#+INDEX: bugbears!"Bugbears" in /Encyclopedia of Magic Creatures/
*/Bugbears/* are a type of magic creature. They are between 8-9 feet
tall, and usually have fur, two arms, two legs, a torso, and at least
one head. However, individual bugbears vary by quite a big.
*** Bugbear Culture
Most bugbears live solitary lifestyles, though some manage to
subjugate other creatures to establish small communities. Often,
bugbears will occupy a goblin clan's home and enslave them, but this
is usually beneficial to the goblins, who enjoy the protection such a
large creature brings.

#+INDEX: goblins!"Goblins" in /Encyclopedia of Magic Creatures/
#+TOC: headlines 1 local
*/Goblins/* are a species of /magic/ creature, and one of the oldest
sapient species on the planet. Native to the /Widewoods/, they at one
point settled as far as the /Central Plains/ and /Gloaming/.

Members of the species are between three and four-and-a-half feet
tall, with black hair and skin that varies in hue from blue to green.
They are capable of respirating air with a significantly higher ratio
of carbon-dioxide, and have excellent vision in the dark.

Their small size has led to them being subjugated by many other
species through their history, and that oppression has shaped their
culture.
*** DRAFT Goblin Culture
Goblins have a strong oral history, featuring many folk heroes.

They also have a long hatred of /horses/, so will gang up on them and
eat them.

*/Unusually large ravens/* are an avian species not native to Teraum.
