## Premise

In the town of *Helmet's Dent*, a man named *Nash Celson* is looking
for help. Bandits have taken over the small castle that's been in his
family for generations, and he wants it back.

The twist: the castle is actually occupied by its rightful owners, the
Culver family. Nash Celson is the one whose claim is illegitimate.

**Adventure question:** Why won't the local law help Celson?

Set in the year 80pc, during the Reconstruction Era.
