Ack is a city of walls within walls. Each ward was once a separate fortification; as the city grew, the walls between them became streets. The oldest ward — the Brass Ward — sits at the center, ringed by newer districts that grew outward like tree rings.

The Chandler's Guild runs the city in all but name. The old nobility lost power after the Rupture, and the merchants filled the vacuum. The Guild hall on Bank Hill is where the real decisions get made.
