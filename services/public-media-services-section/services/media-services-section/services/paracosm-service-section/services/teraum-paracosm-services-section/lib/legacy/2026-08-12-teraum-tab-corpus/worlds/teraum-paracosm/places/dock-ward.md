visual-description: |
  This is the Dock Ward, the oldest district in the city of Ack. Originally settled by humans retiring from a life fishing the Optic Ocean, the district is a hub of import and export. The blocks are filled with warehouses, and the streets are nearly blocked by the workers tent camps. Around the district is an unusual wooden wall. It was constructed by rolling decommissioned ships on their side, helping to protect against the militant tribes that then roamed the Central Plains.

  A bridge from here leads north across the murky brown Green River toward the North Ward. The Kingsroad cuts east and west through the district, leading to the Copper Ward and the harbour, respectively. Heading south through the maze of tents and warehouses leads to the Squash Ward.
