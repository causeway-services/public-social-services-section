name: "Teraumic calendar"
description: "Teraum's calendar is 365.25 days long. Twelve months of 30 days (organized as three ten-day periods each), grouped into four three-month seasons. Five (sometimes six) annual holidays end the seasons (with a sixth, Restyg or Shieldmeet, every four years for leap). Three canonical naming systems exist for the months — the older agricultural calendar (Sleeting/Frosting/Snowing/etc.), the MASH timeline (Deepwinter/Flowers/Highsun/etc.), and the Red Union calendar — which mostly agree on structure but disagree on month names and holiday placement."
summary: "12 months × 30 days + holidays = 365.25 days. Year starts 1 Sleeting/Deepwinter (Jan 1)."
lore: |
  Timekeeping on Teraum is the business of tracking time and days. Time is divided into years, each with 12 months containing three ten-day periods. There are five (sometimes six) annual holidays for a year that is 365.25 days long. The calendar was originally established by humans in the Green Delta to coordinate agriculture; it follows that region's weather. Year-start is 1 Sleeting (= 1 Deepwinter = Earth's January 1 = the date of the Collapse, 0pc).

  Three canonical naming systems exist:

  ## The agricultural / old-reference calendar

  | Season | Month 1   | Month 2  | Month 3     | Holiday(s)                          |
  |--------|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------------------------------|
  | Winter | Frosting  | Snowing  | Sleeting    | Wintertyg                           |
  | Spring | Winter    | Melting  | Showering   | Springtyg                           |
  | Summer | Blooming  | Summer   | Droughting  | Summertyg (& Restyg, every 4 years) |
  | Fall   | Browning  | Dusking  | Rotting     | Harvestyg & Feastyg                 |

  ## The Red Union calendar

  Same year structure but different month names. Holidays renamed and re-placed:

  | Month / *Holiday* | Earth equivalent | Description |
  |---|---|---|
  | Deepwinter        | January   | Most places frozen except Gnalens (mid-50s) |
  | *Midwinter*       | Christmas | Religious holiday; second gift-giving |
  | Teraumian Winter  | February  | Most places frozen except Gnalens and Halfling Country |
  | Sunsets           | March     | **Spring equinox: 19 Sunsets**. Ack thaws |
  | Storms            | April     | Central Plains thaw |
  | *Greengrass*      | Easter    | Planting festival; gorging on the last reserves of winter |
  | Melting           | May       | Farsteppes thaw |
  | Flowers           | June      | **Summer solstice: 20 Flowers** |
  | *Summertide*      | July      | Farfarsteppes thaw |
  | *Midsummer*       | —         | Every 4 years, *Shieldmeet* follows Midsummer (leap day) |
  | Highsun           | August    | Farfarsteppes harvest. **Constructive Criticism Day: 15 Highsun** |
  | Fading            | September | **Autumn equinox: 21 Fading**. Farfarsteppes freeze |
  | *Highharvesttide* | Thanksgiving | Gift-giving, big feast — everyone celebrates |
  | Leaffall          | October   | Farsteppes freezes |
  | Rotting           | November  | Central Plains freeze |
  | *Feast of the Moon* | —       | Various regional celebrations |
  | Drawing           | December  | **Winter solstice: 20 Drawing** |

  Per the Red Union: "They're called holidays, holy-days, because the Red Union is salty about the divine origins of their environment and the many ways it complicates their systems, like a clean 30-day, 12-month calendar."

  ## MASH timeline naming (current canon)

  Uses these month names: Deepwinter, Winter, Melting, Flowers, Blooming, Greengrass, Highsun, Sunsets, Storms, Rotting, Leaffall, Drawing. Note that Greengrass appears as both a month and a holiday across systems.

  ## Equivalences (best-guess)

  - Sleeting → Deepwinter (the Red Union and MASH both use Deepwinter; the older calendar's Sleeting is its predecessor)
  - The other older names (Frosting, Snowing, Showering, Summer, Droughting, Browning, Dusking) lack clean equivalents and may be retconned away

  ## Year suffixes

  - **bB** (before Break) — current standard
  - **aB** (after Break) — current standard, paired with bB
  - **bc / pc** — older variants for bB / aB (retained in some MASH timeline entries)
  - **bR** (before Rupture) — encyclopedia uses this for some year-entries; Rupture is alternate name for the Break/Collapse
  - **rw** (real-world / out-of-character / authorial)
