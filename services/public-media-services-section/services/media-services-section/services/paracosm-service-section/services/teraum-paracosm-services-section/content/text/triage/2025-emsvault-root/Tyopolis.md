Formerly ***Tayopopopolis***, the name got abbreviated by [[trader|traders]].

Tyopolis is a [[city]] located in the [[Unseen Sea]]. Originally created by magic users, the place is reliant on a slowly depleting subterranean aquifer and complex architecture to deliver water and cool air. Based loosely on [[Al Hambra]], in [[Granada]], but also [[Aleppo]], [[Baghdad]], and [[Qom]].
