Tag: Teraum.

So I've been tinkering with Azgaar's Fantasy World Generator and I think I've made a base that I'd like to start building my world around. I have a break between obligations, so I'm going to take the time to share a bit about the area I think I'll be focusing the Teraum MUD (pending potential renam) will start with: Johara settlement, Abah County, Kingdom of Tuged: southern foothills of the Meri mountain range, on the more massive continent.

  • Built into a small temperate decidous forest (known as Joharaw Forest) approximately 2000ft up the Meri mountains, this settlement is home to ~350 human people.
  • The Lortuzian Sea to the south causes frequent showers through the spring, summer, and fall, keeping the forest cool even through the summer. -The cooler temperatures and frequent precipitation has led to the community cultivating variety of a potatoes and brassicas.
  • The settlement is permanent, with a small local iron mine.
    • Buildings are constructed as A-frames out of local hardwood.
    • There is enough of a charcoal industry to sustain the forge and community buildings.
  • The settlement is about 20 miles north of the nearest major road
    • About 60 miles from the county seat, the coastal city of Abah
    • So , it's relatively self-sufficient and doesn't routinely engage in trade.
  • The iron mine used to be more productive, but an earthquake 16 years prior that shook the Meri mountains has discouraged locals from mining.
    • The earthquake killed 22 residents of Joharaw who were working in the mine.
  • There is a hotsprings in the forest, approximately 5 miles northwest (further up the mountain).
  • There is a pass through the Meri mountains due north of Johara.
  • Ecology of Johara Forest
    • Animals
      • Eagles
      • black bear (1 per square mile, so maybe two families?)
      • platypus
      • chipmunk
      • squirrel
      • dormouse
    • Plants
      • Elm
      • Oak
