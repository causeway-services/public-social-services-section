\section*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Belcaer Gazetteer}
\label{sec:org8b84f33}
This gazetteer is a \emph{draft}: it is unfinished and that should be considered when reading it. Special attention should be paid to text marked DRAFT, EDIT, or TK.

This gazetteer is part of a larger project, Teraum. Teraum is a tragically funny fantasy world to be implemented as role-playing game resources, stories, and radioplays. Teraum is a fantasy world in early and active drafting stages being created by emsenn. For more information, see \href{https://emsenn.net/teraum/}{https://emsenn.net/teraum/}
\subsection*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Overview}
\label{sec:orgbaaa67e}
\begin{quote}
It's fortunate that mesa is there. Without Belcaer, there wouldn't be
a notable town for miles. And I'd hate to think of what folk would get
up to back in those fens if there weren't any sort of guard,
considering the mischief we've got now.
\end{quote}

\textbf{\emph{Belcaer}} is a small town situated on a limestone mesa overlooking
the trade road between \emph{Ack} and \emph{Tayopopopolis}, two distant cities.
It is in a region known as the \emph{Gloaming}, where miles of freshwater
marshland give the land a permanent pallour.

There are about 400 residents. Most work in retail or service in town.
\subsection*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} History}
\label{sec:orgae79204}
\subsubsection*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Founding of Belcaer}
\label{sec:org20f21ce}
\index{223bc!"Founding of Belcaer" in /Belcaer Gazetteer/}
The town of Belcaer was founded in 223bc by humans traveling through
the region, between the \emph{Central Plains} to the north and \emph{Unseen Sea}
(actually a desert) to the south. For many decades it was only a
collection of inns and houses built next to the mesa.
\subsubsection*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Contemporary Belcaer}
\label{sec:org0bc4159}
The prosperity of the Green Delta in recent years has increased how
many people are traveling through the Gloaming, and Belcaer has begun
to grow. Unfortunately, the limited size of the mesa on which the town
is built is requiring some creative construction, like the recently
constructed \emph{Guildhouse}.

Crime followed prosperity. Not only are there more bandits on the
road, but an increase amount of smuggling and other crime is occuring
up in the town.

\subsubsection*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Streets}
\label{sec:org9bdf985}
\paragraph{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} High Street}
\label{sec:orgadb8d5a}
\index{High Street!High Street in Belcaer!"High Street" in /Belcaer Gazetteer/}
\textbf{\emph{High Street}} is Belcaer's main street. It leads from the trade road
up the northeastern side of the mesa. At the base of the mesa, the
road is built from piled earth, while as it rises it becomes cut into
the mesa. At the top, it leads south, and many of the town's stores
and taverns are built on it.

Near where high street meets the \emph{trade road}, there is an informal
marketplaces. Here, passing traders make deals with each other, hoping
to make the most of their journey. A person who \textbf{risks} a day shopping
may find something quite valuable.
\paragraph{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Trade Road}
\label{sec:org0cb2dd6}
\index{Trade Road!Trade Road in the Gloaming!"Trade Road" in /Belcaer Gazetteer/}
The \textbf{\emph{trade road}} is a road that cuts roughly north-south through the
\emph{Gloaming}, between the \emph{Central Plains} and \emph{Unseen Sea}.

Belcaer is situated next to it, about halfway through the region.
\subsubsection*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Buildings}
\label{sec:org6d2b907}
\paragraph{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Kerr Stoneworks}
\label{sec:org179e3ff}
A complex of connected stone buildings near the middle of town, \textbf{\emph{Kerr
Stoneworks}} is one of the town's few industries. Here, \emph{Gerdga Kerr}
handles the mining and export of \emph{limestone} that she quarries from
inside the mesa. Recently, the \emph{Red Union} has been pressuring her to
export even more limestone.
\paragraph{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} The Guildhouse}
\label{sec:orgfaa06c5}
\index{Guildhouse!Guildhouse in Belcaer!"The Guildhouse" in /Belcaer Gazetteer/}
The \textbf{\emph{Guildhouse}} is one of the largest, most recent, and most
distinct buildings in town. It was constructed in 76pc from pine
harvested from the region. It juts over the lip of the mesa, supported
by an intricate latice of timber beams.

\subsubsection*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Abeth Harbrook}
\label{sec:orgb59bad2}
\index{Harbrook, Abeth!"Abeth Harbrook" in /Belcaer Gazetteer/}
\textbf{\emph{Abeth Harbrook}} is the senior representative of the \emph{Chandlers
Guild} in town. She is not from, nor does she enjoy, the town or
region.
\subsection*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Plots}
\label{sec:org0128942}
\begin{itemize}
\item The \emph{Red Union} is pressuring \emph{Gerdga Kerr} to export more limestone
to \emph{Ack}. To do so, she'll have to either mine mesas outside of
town, which could be attacked by bandits, or mine under the town,
which could cause it to collapse.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{{\bfseries\sffamily DRAFT} Index}
\label{sec:org750d5ae}
\begin{description}
\item[{Ack}] TK
\item[{Belcaer}] A small town populated by humans in the \emph{Gloaming}. The
subject of this gazetteer.
\item[{Bark, Idbol}] The leader of a small group of outlaws who extort a nearby trading town.
\item[{Central Plains}] A large agricultural region settled by humans.
\item[{Chandlers Guild}] A \emph{human} commercial organization that involves itself in everything from apiculture to newspapers.
\item[{Gloaming}] A region where marshes and mesas give home to bandits and beasts.
\item[{Green Delta}] A region densely settled by humans, prosperous and cultured.
\item[{Guildhouse}] A recently-constructed pine building on the eastern
edge of Belcaer. See \hyperref[sec:orgfaa06c5]{"The Guildhouse"}.
\item[{Harbrook, Abeth}] The senior representative of the \emph{Chandlers
Guild} in Belcaer. See \hyperref[sec:orgb59bad2]{"Abeth Harbrook"}.
\item[{High Street}] The main street of Belcaer, which starts at the
\emph{trade road} and runs south through town. See
\hyperref[sec:orgadb8d5a]{"High Street"}.
\item[{Tayopopopolis}] A city in deep in the desert, isolated and innovative.
\item[{Trade Road}] The main road through the \emph{Gloaming}, the only route through the region.
\item[{Unseen Sea}] A vast desert of rolling dunes and salt flats.
\end{description}
