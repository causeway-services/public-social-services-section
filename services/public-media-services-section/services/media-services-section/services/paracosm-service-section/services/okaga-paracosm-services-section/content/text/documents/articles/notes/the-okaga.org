*** DRAFT Scene 1                  :Hotah:Skah:@Horticulture_Reception:
Skah's[fn:2] heel squeaked as she stopped short before the bend in the corridor, and again as she resumed jogging, after making sure the way was clear. The call from Horticulture had been urgent.

As she went down the way, the lighting began to shift toward that unique purple that characterized the section. Her shoe squeaked again as she stopped at the door, and she took a big breath in as she opened the door.

"Hotah!"[fn:3] she said, surprised to see him sitting behind the desk. "I thought you were on runner duty like me?"

"Horticulture's trained staff are all busy, so they pulled me away. We're coming up on Ben[fn:4] faster than expected, so they want to get starters going now. I've got an inventory for you to pull from Storage, here." Hotah slid a clipboard across the desk. Skah reached out to take it.

"Doesn't seem like it needed an 'urgent,'" she commented, skimming the listing. "A lot of apples."

"Some blight on their largest planet," Hotah explained. "Anyplace you're planning to visit when we arrive?"

"Orchards, I guess," Skah replied, smiling. "And you know I'll visit the recreation complexes on Ben-Four. The immersim just can't do those jumps justice."

"Let me know your schedule, I'll come with you."

"Of course. Well," Skah waved the clipboard in her hand. "Better get to this 'urgent' work," she said, sighing with a smile. Hotah grinned back, and turned his attention to the digital display on his desk.
*** DRAFT Scene 2                                :Skah:@Receiving_Five:
Skah didn't jog as she made her way from Horticulture to the nearest receiving station, and when she arrived, there was a small group of other crew waiting to use the system. She grabbed a ticket, #76, and looked at the station's display, "69." Nice: not to long of a wait. She went to the bar and smiled at the person waiting behind.

"What can I get for you?" he asked.

"Fruit juice?" she asked, and he nodded, working behind the bar for a moment before setting a glass full of opaque pink juice on the counter.

"Grapefruit and apple," he informed her. /Apple sort of day,/ Skah thought to herself, smiling at the person who served her and taking her glass to a table. Someone was playing the piano at the station, but just as Skah sat down, the station's display made a buzz and switched to "71," and the piano-player got up and went to the station proper.

Someone's kid, maybe 8, brought themselves up to the piano and after getting comfortable on the bench, starting hammering classic melodies played without rhythm. /Sounds out of time/, Skah thought, amusing herself with the wordplay.

The child played for several minutes, before the person with ticket #73 scooped them up and brought them to the corridor, where a handtruck loaded with boxes was waiting. They went down the hall, and Skah returned her glass to the bar. Someone else was standing behind it now, and she smiled at them as they took the glass away.

The station display buzzed again, "76," so Skah went up to the station proper, and looked at her clipboard. She began to punch in the requisition numbers, pressing a button labeled "REQ" after each number. By the time she was on the 20th request, small envelopes began to drop from a chute in the station.

All-in-all, Skah requested 83 envelopes, each containing about two dozen seeds. When she'd received them all, she stepped to the side, and pushed another button on the station. The display buzzed, "77."

Skah grabbed a satchel from the station, and put her envelopes in it as she slung it over her shoulder: a familiar motion. She walked almost back to the corridor, before stopping and returning to the bar.

"Biscuit?" she asked the person behind it, who handed her a scone.

"Blueberry," he said, and she smiled in thanks, before heading on her way back to Horticulture.
*** DRAFT Scene 3                                      :Dapu:@Apotei_Orchard:
Dapu[fn:1] stepped back from the line of trees. /At least forty,/ they thought to themself, looking up and down the line. After a pause, they angrily kicked their toes into the soil, sending dirt and dandelion seeds flying.

At least forty of the trees in this section of the Apotei Orchard[fn:5] had scorched leaves mixed in with the healthy growth. It created the apperance of a very selective drought: individual branches had been chosen to be browned and withered away.

Dapu sat on a grassy knoll built near the edge of the grove and appreciated their fortune. Though the orchard's plants might be blighted, and the Stryptomicin sample at the associated pharmacy is contemplated, the Okaga was just a few weeks away.

They only wished there was a way to put in a request with the ship now. If Dapu knew the orchard could get a supply of antibiotics, then the blighted trees wouldn't need to be removed. If they clear the trees and can't get new rootstock, it'll hurt production.

Dapu looked at the sky. Just a few weeks.
*** DRAFT Scene 4                               :Skah:Hotah:@Convention_Hall:
"Five rows!" Skah cheered out as the chair slid across the Convention Hall floor into Hotah's waiting hands. He pushed it down the row and slid it into place, returning to the aisle.

Skah and Hotah, along with a few others, were setting up chairs for the Arrival Convention, and were competing over who could push the Delegates' rolling chairs further without them toppling or crashing into a table.

"Getting closer!" Hotah called back as he caught the next chair. On the previous aisle, he'd managed to make it one row further.

Skah looked at the stage over Hotah's shoulder, and the large monitor at its back. It displayed a starscape. In the center was one star, not as bright as others but larger than any, a focused yellow dot. They'd be arriving in the Ben system soon.

"This will be your first Convention, won't it?" Hotah asked, noticing her distraction. It was a polite way of asking what he meant: /This will be your first time working in a system, won't it?/

Skah hadn't felt this anxious about being on a part of the galactic patrol since she'd boarded the Okaga, 12 years earlier. Before that, she'd never been, out of her home system. She'd never even been off-plant except for vacations with her grandparents, who loved going to its moon. But her aunt had seen more than a half-dozen systems by serving in the galactic patrol. And her aunt returned home with stories of those systems just as Skah was becoming an impressionable teen. Tales of strange cultures and wonderous landscapes gave Skah a direction, and when the Okaga came into the system, she joined as soon as she could.

She knew when she joined it'd be at least ten years before the ship arrived in the next system, but she still spent her first nights restless, imagining walking under the light of different suns.

As she threw herself into the curriculum, her nerves faded, and by the time she'd completed her general training, she'd fallen into a comfortable routine aboard the ship.

Now, looking at that yellow sun, Skah could feel all the nerves she carried when she first boarded.

"Yeah. First time," she said, shoving another chair. It clipped the first desk and toppled over. Hotah jogged up and uprighted it, making a point to look Skah in the eyes and smile at her. He'd been to two systems before hers, making him one of the more experienced memebers of the crew.

"It'll go just one step at a time. First we'll have the Convention. Ben gets fairly regular patrols, so that should go smoothly - my first system..." Hotah trailed off. "I'll tell you about that after this one, which should go smooth. Then you'll get assigned to one of the committees the Delegates choose to form, and you'll get shipped off somewhere in-system to... build houses or a bridge or whatever," he said, pushing the chair into position.

"Whichever and whatever," Skah replied, smiling and shoving another chair down the aisle.
*** DRAFT Scene 5                                      :Dapu:@Buinga_Station:
They hadn't realized it until stepping into the carriage, but it had been nearly a year since Dapu had gone further from the Orchard than their Union Hall. There hadn't been much need, thinking back on it.

Now they were going a lot further than the Hall. This train would take them to Buinga Station, where craft from the Okaga were waiting to take delegates like them to the ship, currently in orbit of Ben-Three.

Dapu looked around at the other passengers. Many wore similar armbands, indicating which group they would be representing at the Convention, where delegates from all over the system would negogiate for what the Okaga brought: both supplies and colonists. Dapu's own armband was green-and-grey, with the text "B2-NSC#71D," which translated to "the fourth Agricultural Nursery Union of the North Sanazi Confederacy on Ben-Two."

Failing to find a familiar face, Dapu slid their luggage under an open window seat and settled down for the first leg of the trip.

Delegate mingles with crew, hears about coodination between in-system communities and Okaga in weeks preceding arrival, that they were excluded from.

Aboard the Okaga, delegates are recalled and others appointed, forcing the Okaga to stay in the system longer than scheduled. (Should the Delegate and Skah/Hotah fall in love?)
*** PLAN Scene 10
The Okaga is robbed: a hole is punched in its sail while delegates from the surface sabotage the receiving stations to send stuff out the hole.
*** PLAN Scene 11
The Okaga uses its small crafts to try and chase the pirates, but the pirates use the signalling array to leave the system, and saboteurs destroy the array stations before the crafts can follow.
*** PLAN Scene 12
Convention Hall. The caucus which voted for the Okaga to stay are accused by the other caucuses of coordinating the robbery. Delegates caught sabotaging the receiving stations are dragged in. People are debating what to do when the Okaga crew comes in.

They announce that the supplies they carry to repair the relay station have been taken, and they have no immediate way to repair them.

In the chaos following the announcement, one of the delegates - the one followed in earlier scenes - is lynched in the convention hall. The Okaga crew then proceeds to detain and deport all in-system residents from the ship.
** PLAN Act 2
*** PLAN Scene 13
It is thirty years past the robbery of the Okaga, and there is a blight affecting apples on Ben-Two. The cooperative controlling the orchards has little faith that a Confederation patrol ship is going to come, and even if they do, they won't know about the existence of the blight. The cooperative is in radio communication with the Okaga, still hung in orbit of Ben-Three, and the cooperative is trying to convince them to bring seeds out of storage. The Okaga is resistant, because they are concerned plants from those seeds will be blighted, too.
** PLAN Act 3
Act 3, it’s a long while after the sabotage, the Okaga has been dismantled and its parts and crew integrated into the system’s own culture. However, without support from the galactic community, there are large blights affecting primary crops, and other issues caused by basically homogeneity and monocultures. The society resolves this by applying the confederacy’s means of organization on a smaller level, encouraging cultivation of local crops and other decentralized research into themselves.
** PLAN Conclusion
n the conclusion, a patrol ship finally pulls into the system, and is amazed to find a society that is doing really well, relative to others. It brings up questions about whether the Confederation is really doing good work by propping up these systems, if it needs to change its methods, etc. It looks like the government of the confederation is willing to change itself, immediately, based on these observed benefits. At the same time, there is a heavy interest in exploiting this system for its new crops and appropriating their innovations for the benefit of the Confederacy, as-is. The story ends on these contrasting tones.

The /Human-Confederacy Ship Okaga/ is a large and relatively modern interstellar vessel operated by the galactic human confederacy. Picture a large dinner plate operating as a solar sail, with a rotating tube around the rim which provides habitable space. It is propelled by light signalling arryas which are built in populated stellar systems.

There are tubes for moving people and goods between different segments of the tube.
