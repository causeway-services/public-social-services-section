# Earth

A paracosm of stories grounded in the real Earth — folktales, fictions,
and other narratives that take place in actual locations and reference
actual people, but with a paracosmic frame: they are part of a
sustained imaginative engagement with real-world settings rather than
detached realism.

Unlike sibling paracosms ([emsean-paracosm](../emsean-paracosm/),
[hevi-mala](../hevi-mala/), [durham-2030](../durham-2030/)) which
invent secondary worlds or fictionalize specific locations, this
paracosm holds stories that work *in* Earth as we know it.

Currently a seed: one folktale entry. Add more as they surface.

## Layout

- `texts/` — Earth-set folktales, fictions, narrative essays.
- `places/`, `beings/`, `things/`, `terms/` — to be created when
  individual entries surface.
