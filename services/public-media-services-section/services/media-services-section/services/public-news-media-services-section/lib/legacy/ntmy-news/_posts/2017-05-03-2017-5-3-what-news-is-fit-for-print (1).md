Managing a news source has always been an effort in managing priorities. When the news was printed, priority was defined by how much space was on the page. Now, priority is defined by market sizes and attention spans, but still, priority is core to deciding what to run.

With _NTMY News_, and our structure of turning Issues into Articles, we aren't able to have a strictly defined process for what is worth running. It's explicitly up to what gets built by the community around the website.

Should we integrate content curation into our qualifying process, or should anything that meets the technical standards be syndicated?

Weigh in on our [Issues](https://github.com/samjoneshouse/ntmy-news/issues/14) board.
