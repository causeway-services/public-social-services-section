# This is the Koios.News publication settlement

Koios.News is a customer publication and a sovereign settlement. It is not one
ambient Nik Koios session, a generic content-farm runner, the Koios News
Service Section, or an inference provider. Its service to readers is timely
general news with warranted systems analysis, visible sourcing, correction,
and accountable circulation.

## Publication law

- Current-event claims come from Koios-owned source custody obtained across an
  explicit Newscable or source-service boundary. Union prior research may
  bootstrap attention and deepen context; it is not present-tense evidence
  until separately admitted.
- Search demand, attention, advertising, and revenue can select attention and
  measure circulation. None supplies factual warrant or can rescue an
  editorial refusal.
- Systems analysis is applied where admitted evidence warrants it. No office
  forces a collapse, climate, SEO, or other preferred frame into unsupported
  prose.
- LLM providers return prose, images, or bounded tool selections. Koios code
  constructs durable structured acts and independently admits or refuses the
  exact returned bytes.
- Source Desk, Reporter, Editor-in-Chief, and Publication Operations retain the
  separate authorities declared in `lib/management/roster.json`. No model
  session inherits all of them by entering this directory.
- Newscable, Corpus Management, Newswire Editorial Production, Independent
  Editorial Assessment, LISS, software development, Git/I/O, and delivery are
  separately contractable services. Koios records a missing service instead of
  performing it under an invented provider identity.

## The issue boundary

Routine publication begins only when the settlement receives one externally
authored ActivityStreams `Create<NewsroomIssueDemand>` under
`koios.news/publication-issue-pulse/v1`. The exact policy admits the trigger
actor and attenuated capability. The publication-local executable never
authors a pulse, claims to be a scheduler, or mints an external pulser actor.

`pnpm run issue:pulse` consumes one activity from stdin and returns one durable
`Accept` or `Reject`. A typed refusal is a successful settlement, not a process
failure. `pnpm run newsroom:pulse` consumes the same external activity and may
continue an accepted assignment through customer-side newsroom contracts. A
separate pulser or scheduler must be formed and contracted before unattended
cadence can be claimed.

The issue controller may select or resume at most one assignment. It cannot
draft, edit, approve, publish, deploy, or select an inference model. A
publication edition is live only after Publication Operations receives exact
editorial admission and an independently witnessed delivery result.

## Interactive work

An interactive agent in this directory is contributed labor inside the office
or bounded service actually named by its grant. Conversation does not make it
the Editor-in-Chief or the publication scheduler. The older skills under
`working/`, memories, and development journals are prior-art and exceptional
manual instruments; they do not define the routine operating path.

Use the publication's append-only ledgers for operational acts. Preserve
source/claim custody, assignments, manuscripts, editorial judgments,
corrections, provider usage and refusals, deployments, attention, advertising,
revenue, and costs in their owning records. Never rewrite another settlement's
receipt or use a Markdown narrative as evidence that an operation ran.

## Recursive improvement

After a terminal outcome, the newsroom learning pulse identifies the earliest
defective layer from owned evidence. Koios may issue a provider-neutral bounded
repair demand, verify the returned change through the owning provider, and
replay the affected work. Repeated successful coordination may be proposed as
law, duties, skills, scripts, or services; it is not silently centralized in
the publication runner.
