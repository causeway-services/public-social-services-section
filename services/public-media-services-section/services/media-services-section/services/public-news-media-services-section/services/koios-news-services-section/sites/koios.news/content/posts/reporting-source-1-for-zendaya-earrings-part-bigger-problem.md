## Epistemic Loss and Permanent Alteration

Unlike restoration efforts that seek to stabilize an object for research, the repurposing of historical artifacts often involves irreversible physical changes, such as drilling or cutting [Zendaya’s Earrings Are Part of a Much Bigger Problem](https://hyperallergic.com/zendayas-earrings-are-part-of-a-much-bigger-problem/).
