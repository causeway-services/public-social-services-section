The U.S. Geological Survey's reviewed [event record for `us6000tkt2`](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/detail/us6000tkt2.geojson) lists a magnitude 7.8 earthquake 64 km NNW of Ende, Indonesia. It gives the event time as 2026-08-14T21:58:21.505Z and the most recent update in the record as 2026-09-01T00:32:09.665Z.[S1]

The same USGS record lists `yellow` in its `alert` field and 7.833 in its `mmi` field.[S1] These are labels and values in the agency's record, not a substitute for reporting from people or authorities at the site.

Its `tsunami` field is `0`.[S1] That is the field value USGS publishes for this event; it is not a broader safety conclusion.

This brief is intentionally narrow. The reviewed record does not, by itself, establish casualties, damage, current conditions, or what may happen next. Those questions require evidence beyond this event-detail entry.
