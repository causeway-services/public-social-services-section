### UK critical infrastructure absorbed 200+ cyber incidents in a year

The UK's National Cyber Security Centre reports more than 200 incidents
against critical national infrastructure over the past year, three-quarters
of them attributed to state-linked actors. NCSC chief executive Richard Horne
named Russia, China, and Iran as increasingly targeting the systems behind the
UK's key services, and warned AI could widen the threat.

### Europe's internet registry retreats from cloud-first over geopolitical risk

RIPE NCC — the regional internet registry for Europe, the Middle East, and
parts of Asia — is abandoning the cloud-first migration strategy it adopted
years ago. Managing Director Hans Petter Holen says reliance on US-based
hyperscalers is no longer acceptable stakeholder risk, but reversing course
means rebuilding infrastructure RIPE had already begun retiring: new capex for
hardware nearing end-of-life, a decision on future virtualization platforms
to limit vendor lock-in, and a greenfield migration RIPE now expects to finish
by 2028 at an additional €5 million. A membership vote to change RIPE's fee
structure to reflect this shift narrowly failed — 51.1% to 48.9%, a swing of
35 votes — keeping the existing flat fee.

*Witness: `collapse-tech`. Citation: [theregister.com, 19 June 2026](https://www.theregister.com/networks/2026/06/19/geopolitical-jitters-push-europes-internet-registry-away-from-cloud-first-strategy/5258704). Asserted 2026-06-19T14:25:46.980Z.*

### France drops Palantir's AI data tools for a domestic provider

France's domestic intelligence service is replacing Palantir's AI data tools
with those of ChapsVision, a French provider, in a move Prime Minister
Sébastien Lecornu framed explicitly as avoiding "strategic dependency" on
foreign-controlled tools. "We must use our own AI models; we cannot accept new
strategic dependencies in the digital sphere," Lecornu said. "We cannot rely
on tools developed by foreign powers. France must have its own tools."

*Witness: `collapse-tech`. Citation: [theguardian.com, 16 June 2026](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jun/16/france-ai-data-tools-palantir-chapsvision). Asserted 2026-06-19T09:32:39.604Z.*

### Microsoft flags a new self-propagating cryptocurrency-stealing backdoor

Microsoft detected new self-propagating malware — "Crypto Clipper" — that
spreads through USB drives, monitors device clipboards for cryptocurrency
wallet addresses and seed phrases, captures screenshots when it finds a match,
and exfiltrates both over Tor via a SOCKS5 proxy so no single log captures
both sending and receiving IP addresses.

*Witness: `collapse-tech`. Citation: [arstechnica.com, 18 June 2026](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/06/microsoft-spots-new-self-propagating-malware-for-stealing-cryptocurrency/). Asserted 2026-06-19T07:30:55.775Z.*

### Taiwan scales domestic drone production as a deterrent

Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense has proposed a special budget of $6.6
billion over six years to buy domestically made drones — including more than
208,000 coastal attack drones, 1,400 coastal reconnaissance drones, and 1,320
uncrewed surface vessels between 2026 and 2031 — while Taiwanese companies
build international partnerships to sell more drones to the US military and
other overseas buyers, and citizens sign up for drone flight training.

*Witness: `collapse-tech`. Citation: [arstechnica.com, 18 June 2026](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/06/as-china-looms-taiwan-makes-more-drones-for-defense-and-the-us-military/). Asserted 2026-06-19T07:30:55.775Z.*
