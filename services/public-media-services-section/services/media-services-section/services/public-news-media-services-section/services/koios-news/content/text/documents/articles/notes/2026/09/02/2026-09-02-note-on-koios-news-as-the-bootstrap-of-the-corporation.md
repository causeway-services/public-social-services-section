## 1. What Koios is for

Koios.News is the first customer and proving ground of the news-domain settlement services, not the boundary of their mission ([the section README](../../../../../../../../README.md)). This note reads that sentence at full strength: Koios is the demand engine that pulls the corporation's supply chain into existence, and the corporation is what that supply chain looks like once every step is bought from ourselves and receipted. The owner's stated vision for the org is "sell every compute on its own internal market, buy it from ourselves, track all resources — and the stack that does this is itself the flagship product" (quoted in [the whole image of the 561 Group](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/09/02/2026-09-02-note-on-the-whole-image-of-the-561-group.md), §8). A newsroom is the cheapest place to run that whole loop for real, because a newsroom already consumes every kind of capability the org sells: research, source acquisition, claim extraction, verification, prose, editing, publication, distribution, correction, and monetization. Each of those is a purchase. Each purchase founds or entangles an enterprise. The books that result are the corporation.

The sibling dossier written today, [meeting then beating Guardian-class news quality](./2026-09-02-note-on-meeting-then-beating-guardian-class-news-quality.md), measured the live site against the reference incumbents and named the closest wedge: expose the per-claim store. This note takes the level above it: what the store is, what it is for, and what it drags into being.

## 2. Koios is the settlement device installed on the news

The firm's central device is the distal bypass: over a territory of local records with overlaps, derive the obstruction sheet from the overlaps, classify each disagreement as damage or border, discharge damage at least cost, certify borders as first-class objects, price every erasure, emit everything as certificates ([the distal bypass](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../operations-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/16/2026-07-16-the-distal-bypass.md)). News is exactly such a territory. Sources overlap on the same event and disagree. Some disagreements are damage: one source is wrong, stale, or mistranscribed. Some are borders: two parties genuinely contest what happened, and no honest newsroom can discharge that by picking one. The four-valued carrier the corpus insists on (FOUR: asserted, denied, both, neither) is the minimum that can hold a border without flattening it, and "convergence guarantees and the capacity for sovereignty exclude each other at the type level" (quoted from [the price of saying](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../operations-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/07/16/2026-07-16-the-price-of-saying.md) in the whole-image note §2).

So an article is a certificate emitted over an obstruction sheet, and the obstruction sheet itself is the systems-analysis depth the masthead promises. This is the note's first idea, and it is engineering, not metaphor: every Koios story carries, as a first-class derived artifact, the map of where its sources agree, where they disagree, and which disagreements the newsroom classified as damage (discharged, with the reason) versus border (held, with both witnesses attached). The ⊤-commodity the org already named as its product category, honest priced held contradiction ([561 Group as meme beadworkers](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/16/2026-08-16-note-on-561-group-as-meme-beadworkers.md) §9, per the whole-image note), has its most legible public form here. No incumbent publishes its sources' disagreements as an object. Koios can, because the device that does it is the firm's own.

## 3. The claim store is the product; articles are projections

The science carrier already says the accumulating asset is not articles but "a structured archive of agent-readable claim states, provenance trails, licenses, corrections, benchmarks, and conformance results" ([agent-readable claim infrastructure](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/media-science-services-section/services/news-media-science-services-section/content/text/triage/claim-infrastructure.md)). The engineering carrier gives the shape: a bitemporal event log as substrate, statement-addressable claim documents, a transparency log for per-claim conformance, push-plus-replay for correction propagation, and gated extraction and generation at the editorial boundary ([the claim infrastructure reference architecture](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/media-engineering-services-section/services/news-media-engineering-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-claim-infrastructure-reference-architecture.md)).

What the store is today: one JSON file per article under `sites/koios.news/store/<category>/<slug>.json`, each carrying title, url, and summary claims with witness and citation, and every claim in the corpus at `force: alleges`, `status: supported` ([koios-intelligence-briefing README](../../../../../../../../services/koios-intelligence-briefing/README.md), measured 2026-08-31). Read against the EAVT strata law, that uniformity is a category error the tree already names: rows are level 0 of the braided tower, asserted, saturated, and settled are different strata and must never be stored indistinguishably ([org layering](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../.claude/rules/org-layering.md), EAVT custody). A whole-article summary marked "supported" is a level-0 assertion wearing a level-2 label. The bitemporal log the reference architecture calls for is the strata made real: a correction is a transaction-time event revising a valid-time claim, never an overwrite. The sibling dossier's per-claim endpoint is the right first face of the store. This note's addition is that the store must become atomic and stratified before that face is worth much: decomposition to one assertion per row, force and status read off the row's own settlement history, and a correction that is a row, not a page.

## 4. The market atom and the newsroom atom are the same object

The owner proposed the market primitive as "literally a (token, claim)" (2026-07-18, quoted in the whole-image note §4). The news-topology memo's most stable primitive is "an attributed, time-indexed claim with provenance and editorial status" ([toward a formal news topology](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/media-science-services-section/services/news-media-science-services-section/content/text/triage/news-topology.md)). These are the same object seen from two custodies. That identity is why Koios is the bootstrap and not merely a product: every row the newsroom settles is a unit the internal market can price, and every unit the market prices is a claim the newsroom can publish. The witnessed-field machine (`@red-cup-engineering/witnessed-field-machine-service`, seated under the semiotic computer-system section) intakes corpus lines as witnessed EAVT rows with FOUR charge, and the predicated-inference kernel landed today with evaluate-corpus, derive-verdict, induce-implications, and settle-valuation as operator scripts (repository HEAD, commit f0be196bb00). Koios's store is the org's only real, provenance-bearing, thousands-of-rows corpus. It is the natural evaluation bed for the semiotic language model program: the machine that is meant to replace transformer inference should first be asked to settle the newsroom's own rows. This is the note's second idea, labeled as this note's own: seat the store as the kernel's first corpus, and let the newsroom's verdicts and the kernel's verdicts be compared row by row, with the disagreement itself as the finding.

## 6. Transnational, literally: gates fibered over jurisdictions

The owner's word for the corporation this session is transnational. The corpus has no ruled term for it, and this section is the note's own reading. A Lakota band's corporate culm publishing on the Web is transnational by construction, not by ambition: the polity is Pheží Ocháǧe ([the term](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../561-group-services-section/content/text/glossary/terms/phezi-ochage.md)); the founder's body and the domain registrar sit in the United States; settlement runs on Base mainnet, a jurisdiction-less layer; federation runs through an ActivityPub actor; and the sources of any given story sit in whatever country the event happened in. The corpus's own image for this is the medicine line, a border that changes which law applies while the people crossing remain themselves ([note on the medicine line](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/history-and-archaeology-science-services-section/services/social-history-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-medicine-line.md)).

The editorial-production memo already gives the mathematics: editorial states are an indexed family over a base category of reportable contexts, jurisdictions explicitly among them, and restriction along a narrower jurisdiction is a base morphism, not an edit ([a restricted fiber algebra of editorial production](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/media-science-services-section/services/news-media-science-services-section/content/text/triage/editorial-production.md)). Make that literal. Every claim carries the jurisdiction of its source and every publication carries the jurisdiction of its face; the gate profile is indexed by that pair, because defamation exposure, correction obligations, and source-protection regimes differ across the United States, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and tribal court. A story that clears the gate for one jurisdiction and holds at ⊤ for another is a border, certified as such, not damage. No incumbent newsroom's gates are a fibration over jurisdictions; theirs are a legal desk's judgment applied after the fact. This is the transnational corporation's first product that is only possible because it is transnational.

## 8. What the external seams can mint

Internal sales circulate; only external seams mint. Read against the claim-infrastructure memo's commodity table, the seams Koios can open, in order of nearness to shipping:

1. **The correction and invalidation feed as a subscription.** Corrections travel as ActivityPub Update activities pushed by WebSub, with replay for late consumers (reference architecture §4). The retained actor already exists. An agent that cites Koios subscribes first to what Koios retracts. This is the commodity the memo rates "very high" compounding and no incumbent sells.
2. **Conflict certificates.** The disagreement map of §2 sold as an object: which sources disagree on which claim, classified, with both witnesses. The house forecasting section already consumes Koios claims through the signal-fusion engine (package graph: `@red-cup-engineering/signal-fusion-alpha-engine-service`), so the first buyer is internal and the second is any research desk.
3. **Source-reliability readouts.** Fold the store's own correction history per source domain into a FOUR-valued reliability reading. This is derived from data Koios already owns and nobody else has; it is the source-power model the accountable-newsroom agenda asks for, sold rather than merely used.
4. **Per-claim bundles priced for agents.** The sibling dossier classified x402-priced claims as revival inventory. The org has two live x402 sellers to copy the shape from; the claim bundle is the third.
5. **The method as a hosted operation set.** Metroplat derives CLI, HTTP, MCP, and LSP faces from certified relational operations ([metroplat](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/services/multi-agent-computer-system-engineering-services-section/services/multi-agent-computer-platform-engineering-services-section/services/agentic-enterprise-computer-platform-engineering-services-section/apps/metroplat/README.md)). Koios's gates, packet formation, and correction transaction, exposed through it, are the stack sold as the product: any newsroom hires the settlement device without hiring Koios.

The honest ⊤ on all five, carried from the whole-image note §8: zero-marginal verdicts do not create external demand by themselves; external sales must price the buyer's trust and translation labor, and the non-vacuity bar is three outside wallets transacting more than once with at least one dispute resolved without owner intervention.

## 9. The reader's face of FOUR

Every article ships two derived blocks no incumbent ships: the claim ledger (what this article asserts, each with its source) and the omission ledger (what the reporting does not establish, and why). The omission ledger is already named as a likely artifact in the accountable-newsroom research agenda ([accountable agentic newsrooms](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/media-science-services-section/services/news-media-science-services-section/content/text/triage/accountable-agentic-newsrooms.md)). Both are projections of the licensed editorial state the section already computes deterministically ([contract](../../../../../../../../src/contract-licensed-editorial-state.mjs)); neither requires a model. This is where the masthead's promise of systems-analysis depth becomes visible on the page: the reader sees the settled, the contested, and the unknown as three different things.

## 10. House NLP first, frontier last

The founder's instruction for this program is scripts, the house's own NLP, small agents, and frontier models only where necessary. The tree already holds the pieces. The English fiber's tagger runs the full settlement engine per sentence and yields role frames and cohomology ([english-language-algebra](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../science-services-section/services/information-science-services-section/services/communication-science-services-section/services/linguistics-services-section/services/english-language-algebra/README.md)); the compaction section holds an extractive-first summarizer with erasure accounting ([token compactification](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/computer-engineering-services-section/services/computer-systems-engineering-services-section/services/computer-inference-system-services-section/services/agent-services-section/services/token-compactification-services-section/README.md)); the claim-extraction study fixes which automated checks may sit in a gate and which may only signal ([study of claim extraction and grounded generation](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../engineering-services-section/services/media-engineering-services-section/services/news-media-engineering-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/08/22/2026-08-22-note-on-the-study-of-claim-extraction-and-grounded-generation-engineering.md)).

The discipline this implies, stated as engineering: sentence segmentation, attribution detection (who said what, from the subject role of reporting verbs), modality and hedge detection, numeric-claim detection, exact-quote verification against the acquired source bytes, duplicate-assertion detection, and source-independence measurement are deterministic and run on every packet and every manuscript at zero inference cost. Prose realization stays a market purchase. Independent assessment stays a market purchase but never from the drafter's model lineage, and never as the sole gate, per the reference architecture's binding on self-preference. Two channels count as verification only when their evidence is independent, so the deterministic reading and the model's reading are recorded as separate witnesses and their disagreement is itself a row. Today the section's text processing is a stopword list and a regular-expression sentence splitter duplicated in two contracts ([research packet](../../../../../../../../src/contract-admissible-research-packet.mjs); [licensed editorial state](../../../../../../../../src/contract-licensed-editorial-state.mjs)); the English fiber is not imported anywhere in the section. Closing that gap is the earliest broken edge of the whole program, because every idea above reads rows that this step produces.

## 11. Cadence as the org's clock

Koios's publication cadence is a standing internal order delivered on schedule ([publication cycle contract](../../../../../../../../sites/koios.news/content/contracts/publication-cycle.json)). Under the universal form the owner stated on 2026-09-01, every idle resource rents itself out and the house is its first customer (whole-image note §4). A newsroom on a fixed pulse is the steadiest internal demand the org has: it keeps the market's providers warm, and the capacity between pulses is what gets sold outward. Koios's clock is therefore the market's heartbeat load, and the measured gap between what a pulse costs and what its seams earn is the first honest cognitive-EROEI datum the org can compute, because both sides of it are receipted in one place.
