Christopher Nolan's adaptation of Homer's *Odyssey* posted a global opening weekend that surpassed every previous debut in the director's career, according to reports published July 19 by [the Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/business/story/2026-07-19/christopher-nolan-the-odyssey-box-office) and [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/box-office/the-odyssey-christopher-nolan-biggest-global-debut-1236815981/). Studio estimates placed the worldwide haul at $264.1 million, establishing the film as the highest-grossing opening weekend of Nolan's career, per the Los Angeles Times.

That total cleared the previous record — held by *The Dark Knight Rises* at approximately $249 million worldwide — by roughly $15 million, according to [WinterIsComing](https://winteriscoming.net/the-odyssey-officially-made-box-office-history-christopher-nolan) and [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/07/box-office-global-the-odyssey-1236996979/). Domestically, the film posted $124.5 million — above Universal's own internal estimate of $117 million — which the Los Angeles Times identified as the year's highest-grossing opening weekend for any live-action or R-rated film. [WinterIsComing](https://winteriscoming.net/the-odyssey-officially-made-box-office-history-christopher-nolan) reported the film posted Nolan's single best opening weekend result across 45 international markets.

## Nolan's Best International Opening

Internationally, individual territory records fell across multiple markets. Citing Deadline data, [WinterIsComing](https://winteriscoming.net/the-odyssey-officially-made-box-office-history-christopher-nolan) reported that the film set the director's highest-ever opening weekend in France, Italy, Spain, Poland, the Netherlands, and Brazil. [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/07/box-office-global-the-odyssey-1236996979/) placed the international total ahead of Nolan's previous best overseas opening, which had belonged to *Oppenheimer*.

## R-Rated, Unfranchised, and Expensive

The scale of the debut stands out given the film's profile. [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/box-office/the-odyssey-christopher-nolan-biggest-global-debut-1236815981/) noted the picture is rated R, runs nearly three hours, and carries no franchise affiliation — a combination that typically constrains commercial ceiling. The production budget was reported between $200 million and $250 million, placing it among the most expensive R-rated productions ever made, according to [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/box-office/the-odyssey-storms-box-office-with-a-usd-264-1-million-global-debut-dethrones-the-dark-knight-rises-as-christopher-nolans-highest-film-opening/articleshow/132501487.cms) and the Los Angeles Times — an adaptation of Homer's ancient epic poem produced through Nolan's Syncopy company alongside his wife and producing partner Emma Thomas, according to the Los Angeles Times.

[WinterIsComing](https://winteriscoming.net/the-odyssey-officially-made-box-office-history-christopher-nolan) identified Matt Damon as Odysseus and Zendaya as Athena in the cast. [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/box-office/the-odyssey-storms-box-office-with-a-usd-264-1-million-global-debut-dethrones-the-dark-knight-rises-as-christopher-nolans-highest-film-opening/articleshow/132501487.cms) reported that the casting — which included actors Lupita Nyong'o and Elliot Page among others — drew commentary from conservative commentators before the film's opening. [WinterIsComing](https://winteriscoming.net/the-odyssey-officially-made-box-office-history-christopher-nolan) noted that the opening also set a career record for star Matt Damon, marking his biggest-ever opening weekend as an actor.

## The IMAX Factor

Premium-format ticket sales were a central driver. [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/box-office/the-odyssey-storms-box-office-with-a-usd-264-1-million-global-debut-dethrones-the-dark-knight-rises-as-christopher-nolans-highest-film-opening/articleshow/132501487.cms) described the film as Nolan's first feature shot entirely on IMAX cameras, with IMAX screens dedicated to the picture for three consecutive weeks. The Los Angeles Times reported that IMAX advance sales shattered company records, nearly doubling the prior benchmark, and that 70mm Imax prints comprised roughly 4 percent of Imax-format revenue. Cinematographer Hoyte van Hoytema conducted IMAX camera tests in August 2024, and Nolan evaluated the results approximately two months later, per the Los Angeles Times. IMAX Chief Executive Rich Gelfond told the Los Angeles Times that the format had become an increasingly important part of the studio ecosystem for filmmakers.

The production also relied on practical filmmaking methods. The Los Angeles Times reported that Nolan's team employed animatronics, puppetry, robotics, and a real Viking sailing vessel — including practical effects in the Cyclops sequences — in an approach the paper noted contributed to the film's mystique with audiences.

[WinterIsComing](https://winteriscoming.net/the-odyssey-officially-made-box-office-history-christopher-nolan) cited several conditions behind the strong opening: early worldwide projections approaching $200 million proved conservative when actual results arrived, IMAX and Thursday preview screenings sold out across the country, and the film opened during a relatively quiet stretch of the summer calendar that gave adult audiences fewer competing options. Universal domestic distribution president Jim Orr said the film delivered on every promise, per the Los Angeles Times. The film earned a 95 percent fresh rating on Rotten Tomatoes, placing it among the best-reviewed of Nolan's career, according to [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/box-office/the-odyssey-storms-box-office-with-a-usd-264-1-million-global-debut-dethrones-the-dark-knight-rises-as-christopher-nolans-highest-film-opening/articleshow/132501487.cms).

## Theater Relief and the Competition Ahead

The broader summer box office ran about 7 percent ahead of last year's pace but still trailed pre-pandemic summer 2019 revenue by roughly 9 percent year-to-date, according to the Los Angeles Times, which described the Odyssey opening as a welcome jolt for theaters following a slower stretch. In its third domestic weekend, Moana finished in a distant second place with approximately $19 million, per the Los Angeles Times and [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/box-office/the-odyssey-storms-box-office-with-a-usd-264-1-million-global-debut-dethrones-the-dark-knight-rises-as-christopher-nolans-highest-film-opening/articleshow/132501487.cms), while other Illumination and Universal titles also underperformed.

The film enters its second weekend without new direct competition, per Times of India, though Sony's *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, with Tom Holland and Zendaya, is scheduled to open in late July, according to [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/box-office/the-odyssey-storms-box-office-with-a-usd-264-1-million-global-debut-dethrones-the-dark-knight-rises-as-christopher-nolans-highest-film-opening/articleshow/132501487.cms) and [WinterIsComing](https://winteriscoming.net/the-odyssey-officially-made-box-office-history-christopher-nolan). Whether the film sustains its audience or falls steeply remains the central open question of its theatrical run.
