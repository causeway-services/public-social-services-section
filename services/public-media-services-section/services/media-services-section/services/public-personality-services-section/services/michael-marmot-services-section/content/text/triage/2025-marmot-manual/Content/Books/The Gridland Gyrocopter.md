**The Gridlands Gyrocopter**

**Summary:** _The Gridlands Gyrocopter_ is a modern fable about a young teen named Alex who is transported into a surreal, fractured world that mirrors the alienation and anxiety of their own. Through encounters with curious individuals and strange pockets of peace, Alex learns to navigate a world shaped by an obsession with quantification and disconnected choices. The story invites readers to reflect on the value of relationships, interdependence, and the transformative power of self-efficacy.

**Premise:** Alex, a teen disillusioned by the pressure to succeed and the impossibility of connection in their own world, receives an unexpected visit from a sleek drone. The drone labels it the Gridland Gyrocopter, and Alex thinks, maybe this is one of the projects they talked about online, sending them a prize. Suspicious but intrigued, Alex sits in the seat and launches, before long, finding himself flying over very unfamiliar terrain.

After landing near the Endless Scroll, a field-sized piece of paper that's constantly being shredded and made, and folk walk about writing and reading whatever they want on the paper, Alex is brought to This, That, and the Other That Wasn't. These two judges, and the alternative they ignore, decide Alex is an alternative to be ignored, sending him to the Nowhere that's Everywhere, where he meets the Man Who Owns Everything (because he doesn't believe in owning anything) and other strange characters. Escaping, Alex finds himself meeting more puzzling people, whose perspective on the places they're in seems to shape them into surreal spaces.

Finding himself frustrated and emboldened by the apparent disconnect between his choices and where he goes, Alex begins to question and violate the absurd norms of the places he is in, and finds himself again in front of This, That, and the Other That Wasn't. This time, Alex listens and talks to the Other That Wasn't, learning more about Gridland's history and how people came to feel a surrender to the consequences of their environment as free choice. That it actually started with This and That, and Math came next, with Debt soon to follow, and Debt found Guilt who'd been there forever. Where are all these people now? Working for This and That, who use their differences to take everything new and make sure it's used to keep taking everything new.

Realizing he can simply leave This & That's court, Alex moves back through the places he's been, antagonizing and negating the systems that keep ideas trapped under This and That, until the people of the Gridlands are forced to actually talk to each other and figure things out—something they, at large, view as a horrible burden. They turn against Alex, and he narrowly escapes the Gridlands in the Gyrocopter—toward new adventure, or back home, is unknown.

## Timeline
- **Timeline of Events:**
  - **Arrival of the Gyrocopter:**
    - Alex receives an unexpected visit from a sleek drone, labeled "Gridland Gyrocopter."
    - It is a peculiar flying machine with a seat for a rider and small wheels.
    - Alex wonders if it’s part of a project they had heard about online, perhaps a prize.
    - Suspicious but intrigued, Alex decides to use it.
  - **Launch and Disorientation:**
    - Alex launches the Gyrocopter and becomes disoriented, flying erratically at first.
    - Eventually, Alex gains bearings and realizes they are very high above unfamiliar terrain.
  - **Landing in the Cabbage Field:**
    - Alex lands roughly in a cabbage field, much to the annoyance of its owner.
    - Alex shakily drives the Gyrocopter to the road.
  - **Meeting the Berry Farmer:**
    - Alex meets a berry farmer along the road and offers him a ride.
    - The farmer explains he’s heading to the Endless Scroll to post a notice about his berries.
  - **Introduction to the Endless Scroll:**
    - Alex and the farmer arrive at the Endless Scroll, a field-sized piece of paper constantly being shredded and remade.
    - People walk about writing and reading on the paper. Alex watches the farmer paint his notice.
    - The farmer leaves to prepare for potential customers.
  - **The Pie Filling Debate:**
    - Nearby, a heated debate about pie fillings erupts, leading to chaos on the Scroll.
    - The farmer’s notice is overwritten by the pie filling argument, causing disruption to his plans.
    - Frustrated, Alex walks away.
  - **Encounter with the Bureaucrat:**
    - Alex meets a peculiar bureaucrat carrying a cage of restless, fluttering paper Nothings.
    - The bureaucrat invites Alex to follow him to the **Department of Collected Nothings.**
  - **Visit to the Department of Collected Nothings:**
    - Alex arrives at the desk and asks a question, only to be chided for "trying to make Something out of Nothing."
    - Alex is sent to This, That, and the Other That Wasn’t.
  - **This, That, and the Other That Wasn’t:**
    - The judges decide Alex is an Other to be ignored and send him to the Nowhere That’s Everywhere.
  - **The Nowhere That’s Everywhere:**
    - Alex meets the Man Who Owns Everything (because he doesn’t believe in owning anything) and other strange characters.
    - Each interaction reveals more about the absurd norms of the Gridlands and its people.
  - **Violation of Norms:**
    - Frustrated, Alex begins to question and violate the systems and norms around him, disrupting the status quo.
  - **Return to This, That, and the Other That Wasn’t:**
    - Alex is brought back to the court but chooses to engage with the Other That Wasn’t.
    - The Other That Wasn’t explains the history of the Gridlands:
      - It began with This and That, who categorized everything.
      - Math followed to measure and justify, then came Debt, who brought Guilt, a figure present since forever.
      - Over time, This and That consolidated their power, ensuring everything new was repurposed to sustain their system.
  - **Leaving the Court:**
    - Realizing they can simply leave, Alex walks out of This & That’s court.
  - **Antagonizing the Systems:**
    - Alex revisits the places they’ve been, disrupting and negating the systems that keep ideas trapped under This and That.
    - These disruptions force people in the Gridlands to communicate and figure things out together.
  - **The Backlash:**
    - The people, viewing meaningful dialogue as a terrible burden, turn against Alex.
    - Alex narrowly escapes the Gridlands in the Gyrocopter.
  - **Ambiguous Ending:**
    - Alex flies away—whether toward a new adventure or back home remains unknown.
