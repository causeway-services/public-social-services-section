My family is embarking on a project to practice adaptive regenerative natural resource management on land we've recently purchased. The details of that purchase and our broader goals are discussed in [another post]().

To ensure the work itself isn’t inappropriately influenced by the costs of land ownership, we will generate revenue by creating and sharing content derived from the work—written pieces, images, and audiovisual material—and publishing it on the Web.

Revenue from this content will be allocated toward investments that serve three purposes: generating income to cover ongoing costs, scaling content publishing and curation capabilities, and achieving capital appreciation to accelerate growth. 

Hive will serve as the primary host for this content, with syndication onto other platforms. I’ve outlined the reasoning behind this decision, and how on-chain revenue will be invested, in a [different post]().

Another segment of revenue will come off-chain, through one-time or subscription pay buttons embedded into the published content. This revenue will be invested in more traditional vehicles, with a focus on creating stability, accelerating growth, and maintaining flexibility for future opportunities.

Off-chain revenue will be allocated based on the source of the income, with specific percentages designated for each platform and investment category. The strategy also aligns with distinct roles for Fidelity and CashApp investments:

- **Fidelity Investments**: Designed to generate reliable income for covering recurring costs such as property taxes and infrastructure.
- **CashApp Investments**: Allocated specifically to address incidental expenses that arise during the project.

#### PayPal Revenue

- **100% of PayPal revenue** will be directed into Fidelity investments.

#### Venmo Revenue

- **20% of Venmo revenue** will be allocated to **Bitcoin (BTC)**.
- **80% of Venmo revenue** will be directed into Fidelity investments.

#### CashApp Revenue

- **100% of CashApp revenue** will be allocated to **Vanguard High Dividend Yield ETF (VYM)**. This fund’s income generation provides a financial buffer for incidental project-related expenses.

#### Fidelity Investments Allocation

All funds directed into Fidelity—including those from PayPal, Venmo, and internal reinvestment—will be distributed as follows:

- **18.14% into Fidelity Flex 500 Index Fund (FDFIX)**: A large-cap U.S. equity fund focused on growth.
- **9.04% into Fidelity Flex International Index Fund (FITFX)**: Provides diversification through exposure to international markets.
- **1.53% into Fidelity Flex Mid Cap Index Fund (FLAPX)**: Targets mid-cap U.S. equities with higher growth potential.
- **1.42% into Fidelity Flex Small Cap Index Fund (FLXSX)**: Focuses on small-cap equities with the highest growth potential but increased risk.
- **19.60% into Fidelity Conservative Income Bond Fund (FUEMX)**: A low-risk bond fund offering stability and predictable income.
- **50.27% into Fidelity Municipal Income Fund (FUENX)**: The largest allocation, providing tax-advantaged, steady income through municipal bonds.

---

### Purpose of Allocation

This allocation strategy reflects a balance between income generation, growth, and flexibility, with a clear delineation of roles for the investment vehicles:

1. **Income-Generating Investments**  
    The majority of funds (over 70%) are directed into stable, low-risk investments, such as municipal bond funds (FUENX) and conservative bond funds (FUEMX). These investments are housed within Fidelity and are explicitly intended to generate reliable, passive income for recurring expenses like property taxes and ongoing infrastructure costs.
    
2. **Growth-Focused Investments**  
    A significant portion of funds (approximately 30%) is allocated to equity funds, including large-cap (FDFIX), international (FITFX), mid-cap (FLAPX), and small-cap (FLXSX) funds. These investments capitalize on potential market growth, addressing the limitations of startup capital by accelerating the portfolio’s development.
    
3. **Incidental Expense Coverage**  
    CashApp revenue is fully allocated to the Vanguard High Dividend Yield ETF (VYM). The steady income generated by VYM is intended to address incidental project-related expenses, ensuring financial flexibility for unexpected or irregular costs.
    
4. **Bitcoin Allocation**  
    A small but intentional allocation to Bitcoin reflects its role as a long-term asset, offering potential for appreciation and liquidity without being tied to immediate income generation.
    

---

### Conclusion

This off-chain allocation plan ensures that all revenue sources are strategically deployed to build a sustainable, resilient financial foundation for the project. Fidelity investments serve as the backbone for covering recurring bills and predictable costs, while CashApp investments provide a flexible buffer for incidental expenses. Growth-focused allocations address the need for early-stage acceleration, ensuring that the project remains adaptable and sustainable over time.

By integrating these off-chain investments with the on-chain strategy, we create a balanced approach that supports both the short- and long-term needs of this work, enabling us to focus on the regenerative practices at the heart of this project.
