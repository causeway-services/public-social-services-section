**Summary of Game Mechanics**

The game is a blockchain-based idle game centered on the inevitability of climate collapse. Players engage with the game by acquiring NFTs and participating in the token economy, driven by the extraction and circulation of FALL tokens.

### Core Mechanics:

1. **FALL Tokens**:
    
    - FALL tokens represent emissions and resource extraction.
        
    - NFTs mint FALL tokens into players' wallets. The rate of minting accelerates as more FALL is in circulation (liquid or pooled).
        
    - Adding FALL to a liquidity pool removes it from contributing to the acceleration rate, incentivizing liquidity contributions.
        
2. **NFTs**:
    
    - NFTs are sold for HIVE. The first round of sales funds the initial FALL-HIVE liquidity pool.
        
    - Subsequent sales use half the proceeds to buy FALL and pair it with HIVE in the liquidity pool.
        
    - NFTs have a resilience stat that determines their ability to continue minting FALL as collapse accelerates.
        
    - Bonuses for NFT collections (e.g., redundancy boosts) require players to maintain specific liquidity pool contributions.
        
    - New NFT sets unlock as the total FALL minting rate crosses predefined thresholds.
        
3. **Liquidity Pool**:
    
    - Players add FALL to the liquidity pool to earn more FALL over time and reduce their impact on minting acceleration.
        
    - Pool fees collected by the game are reinvested into the liquidity pool to maintain stability and liquidity.
        
4. **Endgame Mechanics**:
    
    - The game ends when no more FALL can be minted.
        
    - The game account uses staked HIVE (from NFT sales) to buy FALL out of the liquidity pool, aiming to clear it out.
        
    - Once the game account is the sole liquidity provider, it withdraws its stake, finalizing the economy. Any remaining HIVE becomes the creator’s earnings.
        
5. **Automated Reports and Curation**:
    
    - The game generates automated reports about its progression, posted on Hive to earn upvotes and generate revenue.
        
    - The game account curates other projects in the gamefi space, creating synergy and fostering community engagement.
        

### Key Themes:

- The game emphasizes the inevitability of collapse, where players must navigate strategies to prolong their participation.
    
- It showcases the mathematical principles of resource extraction and its impact on production conditions, avoiding reliance on political or theoretical narratives.
    
- The end-state of the game leaves FALL tokens as a symbolic representation of the extraction process and the finite nature of the system.
    

This design ensures a cohesive loop of engagement, progression, and thematic resonance with minimal reliance on centralized applications or complex interfaces.
